# DEC-013 — Decisions on the 2026-11-25 review

Not the predecessor repository's DEC-013 (route states), which is archived
and unrelated. The review and the seven-pass plan are in
`docs/review-2026-11-25.md`.

## 1. The 1824 *Discorsi* governs, for now

The Milan 1824 edition governs because a complete scan of it is freely
available and legible. Neither reason is textual. The first printing is
Blado's (Rome, 1531), and a future edition may rely on it. SCOPE §4, every
Machiavelli source note and the introduction say so.

## 2. Bausi 2001 is treated exactly as Palmarocchi 1933

Its readings may be consulted and reported as facts, with a page reference,
at loci where we emend or where our witnesses split. Its expression (its
apparatus, notes and introduction) is never copied. Neither edition governs
or supplies the established text. An emendation is still made from the
comparison witness under D68.

This replaces the earlier exclusion of Bausi in SCOPE §1 and §8, RIGHTS §4,
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and D38's revisit clause. The edition still
does not establish a critical text and does not adjudicate against the
manuscript tradition.

## 3. Completeness: the conventional reading

The *Considerazioni* are unrevised working notes. The 39 units are the set
that survives, and whether the work is complete is an open question. "He did
not stop early", "the set is closed" and "and stopped" are withdrawn wherever
they appeared.

## 4. Date of composition: one source

One source decides the date, and one wording is used everywhere, citing that
source. Ada Palmer has published nothing on dating the *Considerazioni*, so
the source is a current Guicciardini specialist, chosen and approved by
David before it was applied:

> Emanuele Cutinelli-Rendina, "Guicciardini, Francesco", *Enciclopedia
> machiavelliana* (Rome: Istituto della Enciclopedia Italiana, 2014),
> section "Guicciardini lettore di Machiavelli": Guicciardini "riprende in
> mano i *Discorsi*, non ancora a stampa, tra la fine del 1529 e la prima
> metà del 1530".

The wording is: **dated by Cutinelli-Rendina to between late 1529 and the
first half of 1530.** Pages say "dated by", not "written in", because this
is one scholar's dating, adopted. The same sentence of his calls the
commentary "not entirely carried out". The edition quotes only the dating,
and its position on completeness stays as point 3.

## 5. v1.0 adds scholarship

DEC-012 gains a seventh criterion: secondary literature in the annotations,
a bibliography and a further-reading list (`apparatus/bibliography.md`). A
person checks every citation against a library catalogue and records the
check. Pass 7 does the work. `scripts/state` reports the criterion.

## 6. The rights assessment stays open

RIGHTS §6 stays open for a second reader (pass 2).

## Consequences

The review's pass 1 applies points 1–5 to SCOPE, `AGENTS.md`,
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `RIGHTS_AND_SOURCES.md`, the pairing map, `build.py` (the
source notes, the bylines, and the index and about pages), the introduction
and DEC-012.
