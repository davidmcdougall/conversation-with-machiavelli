# DEC-012 — What v1.0 is

## Issue

The edition is at 0.3 with 38 of 39 pairs published. v1.0 gates the Zenodo
DOI and the downloadable reading copy, but nothing defined it. The only human
check on the English (SCOPE §7: reread a published unit cold three months
later) had never been carried out, and David doubted it was useful.

## Decision

v1.0 is released when all of the following hold. `scripts/state` reports each
one in SCOPE §4 from the files.

1. **All 39 pairs published.**
2. **No open items.** Every O-item in `apparatus/decision-log.md` ruled.
3. **A sample read of the English.** David reads eight pairs against the
   Italian, drawn at random, at least one per book and the rest in proportion
   (five from Book I, two from Book II, one from Book III), and logs each in
   `docs/sample-reads.md`. A real error (not a matter of taste) is fixed, logged
   with its D-number, and adds the next pair from the recorded reserve list to
   the sample. Two failing reads in a row stop the release until the review
   method is reconsidered. This replaces the three-month cold reread.
4. **An introduction.** 1,500–2,500 words in `apparatus/introduction.md`,
   opening with the 1513 letter to Vettori and the argument SCOPE §1 gives it.
   Drafted by Claude, rewritten by David, credited "AI-assisted, edited by
   David McDougall", with the drafting model recorded (DEC-011). Method stays
   on the About page.
5. **PDF and EPUB** of the edition, generated by the build, in
   `site/downloads/`.
6. **A Zenodo DOI at release.** A manual upload of the release without
   `sources/witnesses/`, so the witness extracts stay where they can be
   withdrawn (`RIGHTS_AND_SOURCES.md` §6 item 3). The DOI is recorded in
   `CITATION.cff`.

7. **Scholarship** (added by DEC-013, 2026-11-25). Secondary literature in
   the annotations, a bibliography and a further-reading list, in
   `apparatus/bibliography.md`. A person checks every citation against a
   library catalogue and records the check in the file.

Not in v1.0:

- The I.27 context unit: after the 39 pairs, as D282 already says.
- The page of unanswered chapters (D289): provisionally v1.1. David is
  undecided and leans towards after; revisit before release.
- An outside reader: welcome, not required.

## Consequences

SCOPE §4 carries the criteria and a derived checklist; SCOPE §7's human check
is now the sample read; `TRANSLATION_METHOD.md` records it. The random draw
and its seed are in `docs/sample-reads.md`, so it can be reproduced and cannot
be rerun to a more convenient result.
