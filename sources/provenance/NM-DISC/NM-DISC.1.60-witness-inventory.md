# NM-DISC.1.60 witness inventory

Acquisition-stage record. Two independently printed editions were bounded-extracted: the 1824 volume-1 edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1824-IA`, governing) and Domenico Giglio's 1554 Venetian edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA`, comparison). `FG-CONS-DR-028` remains the authoritative relationship pointer; this record imports no Guicciardini text.

The 1824 edition's page range was cross-validated against this project's established NM-DISC.1.12 ground truth and, for units beyond the first archive.org volume, against independently confirmed chapter headings in the second volume (`discorsisopralap02mach`) — see the witness YAML's `chapter_locator`.

**The 1554 Giglio range is verified.** Re-located on 2026-10-01 from the rendered page images of the full scan (its sha256 matches the hash recorded in `WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA.yml`), not from the OCR layer. The printed heading, "COME IL CONSOLATO, ET qualunche altro magistrato in Roma si daua senza rispetto di eta. Capitolo. LX.", opens zero-based page 201 (fol. 83v); the extract runs through page 203 (fol. 84v), where the next chapter begins. Rubric and chapter number agree with the 1824 witness. The final page is the Book II title and preface leaf, which opens the II.proem extract. This replaces the provisional range 140-145, which was read off the volume's TAVOLA with an assumed "printed page + 58" offset and contained none of this chapter. The volume is foliated, not paginated, so that offset held only at fol. 23v (the I.12 anchor).
