# NM-DISC.2.19 witness inventory

Acquisition-stage record. Two independently printed editions were bounded-extracted: the 1824 volume-2 edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1824-IA`, governing) and Domenico Giglio's 1554 Venetian edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA`, comparison). `FG-CONS-DR-035` remains the authoritative relationship pointer; this record imports no Guicciardini text.

The 1824 edition's page range was cross-validated against this project's established NM-DISC.1.12 ground truth and, for units beyond the first archive.org volume, against independently confirmed chapter headings in the second volume (`discorsisopralap02mach`) — see the witness YAML's `chapter_locator`.

**The 1554 Giglio range is verified.** Re-located on 2026-10-01 from the rendered page images of the full scan (its sha256 matches the hash recorded in `WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA.yml`), not from the OCR layer. The printed heading, "CHE GLI ACQVISTI NELLE REP. NON bene ordinate, ... Ca. 19", opens zero-based page 274 (fol. 120r); the extract runs through page 279 (fol. 122v), where the next chapter begins. Rubric and chapter number agree with the 1824 witness. This replaces the provisional range 176-181, which was read off the volume's TAVOLA with an assumed "printed page + 58" offset and contained none of this chapter. The volume is foliated, not paginated, so that offset held only at fol. 23v (the I.12 anchor).
