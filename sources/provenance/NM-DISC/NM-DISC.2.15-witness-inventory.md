# NM-DISC.2.15 witness inventory

Acquisition-stage record. Two independently printed editions were bounded-extracted: the 1824 volume-1 edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1824-IA`, governing) and Domenico Giglio's 1554 Venetian edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA`, comparison). `FG-CONS-DR-034` remains the authoritative relationship pointer; this record imports no Guicciardini text.

The 1824 edition's page range was cross-validated against this project's established NM-DISC.1.12 ground truth and, for units beyond the first archive.org volume, against independently confirmed chapter headings in the second volume (`discorsisopralap02mach`) — see the witness YAML's `chapter_locator`.

**The 1554 Giglio range is verified.** Re-located on 2026-10-01 from the rendered page images of the full scan (its sha256 matches the hash recorded in `WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA.yml`), not from the OCR layer. The printed heading, "GLI STATI DEBBOLI SEMPRE fieno ambigui nel risoluersi, et sempre le deliberationi lente sono nociue. Ca. XV.", opens zero-based page 252 (fol. 109r); the extract runs through page 256 (fol. 111r), where the next chapter begins. Rubric and chapter number agree with the 1824 witness. Its first page is shared with the II.14 extract, where chapter XIIII ends above this heading. This replaces the provisional range 165-170, which was read off the volume's TAVOLA with an assumed "printed page + 58" offset and contained none of this chapter. The volume is foliated, not paginated, so that offset held only at fol. 23v (the I.12 anchor).
