# NM-DISC.2.proem witness inventory

Acquisition-stage record. Two independently printed editions were bounded-extracted: the 1824 volume-1 edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1824-IA`, governing) and Domenico Giglio's 1554 Venetian edition (`WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA`, comparison). `FG-CONS-DR-029` remains the authoritative relationship pointer; this record imports no Guicciardini text.

The 1824 edition's page range was cross-validated against this project's established NM-DISC.1.12 ground truth and, for units beyond the first archive.org volume, against independently confirmed chapter headings in the second volume (`discorsisopralap02mach`) — see the witness YAML's `chapter_locator`.

**The 1554 Giglio range is verified.** Re-located on 2026-10-01 from the rendered page images of the full scan (its sha256 matches the hash recorded in `WIT-NM-DISC-1554-IA.yml`), not from the OCR layer. The printed heading, "DELLI DISCORSI DI NICOLO MACHIAVELLI ... LIBRO SECONDO. PREFATIONE.", opens zero-based page 203 (fol. 84v); the extract runs through page 208 (fol. 87r), where the next chapter begins. Rubric and chapter number agree with the 1824 witness. Giglio heads the proem PREFATIONE under a Book II title; the 1824 witness prints no proem heading, so the match rests on position (between I.60 and II.1) and on the opening words. This replaces the provisional range 141-156, which was read off the volume's TAVOLA with an assumed "printed page + 58" offset and contained none of this chapter. The volume is foliated, not paginated, so that offset held only at fol. 23v (the I.12 anchor).
