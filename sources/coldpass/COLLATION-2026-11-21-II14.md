## NM-DISC.2.14 — 1824, pp. 262-264

404 words (pass A: p. 262 41, p. 263 254, p. 264 109). Pass A finds
CAPITOLO XIV on p. 262 below the end of II.13 (`... è più coperta, come
fu questa de'Romani.`), the two-line italic rubric `Ingannansi molte volte
gli uomini, credendo / con la umiltà vincere la superbia.`, an enlarged
initial V, one printed block with no indents and no notes, the Latin of
Livy 8.4 in italic (p. 263 ll. 9-15), and the end at p. 264 l. 13 `fia
sempre partito prudente.`, CAPITOLO XV following on the same page.

**The sample (D302).** A fifth of 404 is 81: p. 263 and p. 264 are
eligible, p. 262 (41) not. Word-level differences from the 1554, after
spelling normalisation: p. 262 none; p. 263 seven (`Pretore`, `nisi a
conscientia`, the Italian rendering added after the Latin, `mai un
Principe debbe` / `non debbe`, `non la possa` / `nō poſſa`, `con la paura`
/ `con paura`, `concesso quella` / `quello`); p. 264 four (`avessi` /
`haveſſe`, `riguadagnarselo` / `riſguardarſelo`, `già` / `di gia`, `fia` /
`ſia`). p. 264 is the densest per word (4 in 109 against 7 in 254) and was
chosen and recorded before it went out, with eight line crops of p. 263
covering the seven differences (pass-A lines 8, 14, 15, 17, 18, 23, 24,
28). Pass A has no doubt between words on p. 263 (D203(a)); its four
doubts are spacing and one damaged `possa`.

**Result.** The sample agrees with pass A word for word (107 tokens to
107), and each of the eight crops reads its line as pass A does. Pass B's
four doubts are e crossbars too faint to see (`forze`, `pertanto`,
`volere`, `detto`), each read `e`. It sets an opening parenthesis before
`detto)` that pass A and the crop do not show (pointing, Class A). Clean
(**D458**). With II.13's fifth crop ruled not a disagreement (D457), D302
stands.

What remains is the space inside `a i` at a line end (p. 263 l. 3), full
spaces before two commas (`onorevolmente ,`, `freddi ,`), apostrophe
spacing, and the broken final a of `possa` (p. 263 l. 23), all Class A.

**Against the 1554 (ruled by precedent).** No difference at a D203(b)

## FG-CONS.2.14 — Canestrini, pp. 67-68

157 words each (p. 67 110, p. 68 47). Both passes find the heading
**CONSIDERAZIONE SUL CAPITOLO XIV** in the middle of p. 67 below the end of
the II.13 reply, with no `DEL LIBRO II`; a two-line rubric in smaller type,
`Ingannansi molte volte gli uomini, credendo con la umiltà / vincere la
superbia`; one indented paragraph; the end at p. 68 l. 6 `di poco
momento.`, with a printed stop; and CONSIDERAZIONE SUL CAPITOLO XV
following on p. 68. Canestrini's note (1), keyed after `in altro luogo,`
and printed at the foot of p. 67, `Nel capitolo xxxiii del libro i`, is his
commentary and is not carried; the cross-reference to Discourses I.33 is
made in the commentary. `paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). Pass A marks
12 loci, pass B 17 (3 and 6 in the rubric, 1 and 2 in the note).

**Body: one split.** p. 67 l. 3 `ne' casi ⟨periculosi|pericolosi⟩`: both
readers see an n-shaped letter after the c; A prefers `periculosi`, B
`pericolosi`. Two forms of one word. The scan breaks u/n; the reply spells
the root with u three times (`periculo` ×2, `periculosa`), and the 1933
edition, which prints `pericolosi` here, normalises those too. Read
`periculosi` on the recommendation (**D462**, David).

**A word both read that does not construe.** p. 67 l. 4 `pari a quello
dello inimico`: both read `quello`, the vowel without doubt (B doubting
only the u). Its referent is `le forze tue`; the masculine agrees with
nothing, and the scan breaks e/o. The 1933 edition prints `quelle`.
Emended `quell[e]` under D68 on the recommendation (**D461**, David, as
D456, D200).

**Doubts where both readers prefer the word** (`temporeggiarsi`,
`distinguere`, `hai`, `quando`, and B's `quello`, `tanto`, `consigliò`):
the scan's e/o, t/i, h/b, u/n breaks; readings, as printed, as D398.
`tôrti`, the accent doubtful between circumflex and grave: `tòrti` by
DEC-009, as `tòrre` at FG I.10 (**D460**).

**Against the 1933 edition.** Otherwise forms (`il` / `el` ×3, `periculo`
/ `pericolo` ×2, `periculosa` / `pericolosa`, `Lacedemonii` /
`lacedemòni`, `condizioni` / `condizione`). Pointing: `urtare,` and
`ruina;`, read alike by both without doubt, kept against the 1933
`urtare;`, `ruina,` (D110); `cedere` with no stop either reader sees
before a capital, the 1933 stop supplied (C001, D67); the stop after
`momento`, printed and seen by both, kept (**D460**).

**Witness independence (SCOPE §7).** Neither pass carries a 1933
word-reading where Canestrini differs: both have `quello`, `il`,
`periculo`, `periculosa`, `Lacedemonii`, `condizioni`. At the split each
reader reported the printed n-shape. The passes read Canestrini.

**Heading and rubric.** The heading as printed, without `del libro II`,
in the unit's `heading` front-matter (D443). The rubric's broken letters
(`umiltà`, `vincere`; B also `Ingannansi`, `gli`, `uomini`), both readers
preferring the words, read with Machiavelli's 1824 rubric; the 1933
`umilitá` in the variant note; no stop, as Canestrini's rubrics are
carried (**D463**, as D451).
