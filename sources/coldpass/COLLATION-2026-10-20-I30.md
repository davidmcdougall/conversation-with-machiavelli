# Pair I.30 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together.
Under D302 the NM sample page waited for pass A and the Giglio read, was
chosen by the density of word-level 1554 differences, and went to a fifth
reader alone with line crops of every word-level 1554 difference outside
it. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini page at 1200
ppi (native 600), whole and in halves; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in
thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages against the passes, and checked
the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf images.

The D317 revisit condition (the next `fazione` in either author) was
checked: the word occurs in neither unit. D317 stays held.

## NM-DISC.1.30 — 1824, pp. 111–113

543 words (pass A: p. 111 45, p. 112 262, p. 113 236). Pass A finds
CAPITOLO XXX on p. 111 after body line 15, which closes chapter XXIX (`si
dirà.`), the four-line italic rubric `Quali modi debbe usare un Principe o
una Re-/pubblica ... no per non essere oppresso da quella.`, a large initial
U, one printed block, no notes, and the end at p. 113 line 25 `dai
cittadini romani.`, above the signature line `MACHIAVELLI V. I` / `8`;
CAPITOLO XXXI opens p. 114. Pass A marks two loci in the whole chapter.

**The sample (D302).** Pages eligible at a fifth of the chapter: 112, 113
(p. 111 holds 8%). Word-level 1554 differences after spelling
normalisation: p. 112 six (`s'ei`/`se`, `loro`/`eßi`, `e' si`/`eßi`, `a quel
Capitano, giudicando che`/`a quel Capitano, che giudicando io, ch'ei`,
`l'offendere`/`l'offenderlo`, `prenda`/`prende`), p. 113 three
(`ambigui`/`dubbiosi`, no `tanti` before `uomini virtuosi`,
`rispettivi`/`con rispetto`); p. 111 one (`ne' tempi`/`a i tempi`). Per
word, p. 112 0.023 against p. 113 0.013: **p. 112**, recorded before it was
given out.

With the page went four crops of three to five lines carrying every
word-level 1554 difference outside it: p. 111 body lines 4–6 (`ne'
tempi`), p. 113 ll. 2–4 (`ambigui`), ll. 12–16 (`tntta`, no `tanti`), ll.
18–20 (`rispettivi`). The reader was told only to transcribe every whole
line.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The 293 tokens of p. 112
are identical between the passes after Class A, and so is every whole line
of the crops. Each reader marked a dot before `rimangono` (p. 112 l. 8) as
a probable speck, preferring none; no stop can fall there. Pass B's other
doubts (`s' ⟨ei|ci⟩`, `⟨fare|farc⟩`, `⟨modi|inodi⟩`, a mark before `non`)
have no word for an alternative. 262 words of the chapter (48%) have a
second reader by page, and every word-level 1554 difference has one by page
or crop. The full pass B is not triggered, and the sample rule stands for
the next chapter.

**A printed non-word.** p. 113 l. 14 `adoperandosi tntta la Città`: both
readers see an n, at full and third resolution, on a crisp page; the 1554
prints `tutta` (fol. 46r). Emended `t[u]tta` under D68, **D318**.

**Against the 1554 (D203(b)).** At letters the scans break the word diff
finds one difference of word: .02 `prenda animosamente la parte contraria`
(p. 112, the sample, both readers without doubt), 1554 `prende` (fol. 45v).
`prenda` is the subjunctive the clause wants, beside `faccia`, `tenga`,
`occupi`. Kept, D38, **D319**.

**Raised although no rule requires it.**

- .02 `Ma dico bene a quel Capitano, giudicando che non possa fuggire i
  morsi della ingratitudine, che faccia` (p. 112, both readers): the 1554
  prints `Ma dico bene a quel Capitano, che giudicando io, ch'ei non possa
  fuggire` (fol. 45v), which makes Machiavelli the one who judges and the
  captain the subject of `possa`. The 1824 leaves the gerund's subject to
  the reader. Kept, D38, **D320**; the English follows the 1824 and a
  translator's note gives the 1554.
- .04 `surgeva sempre in Roma in ogni età uomini virtuosi, ... che il Popolo
  non aveva cagione` (p. 113, crop): the 1554 prints `tanti huomini
  uirtuosi` (fol. 46r), which gives the `che` its antecedent. Kept, D38,
  **D321**; variant note.

Not raised, and recorded as variant notes: `quelli Imperadori`/`quei`,
`ne' tempi`/`a i tempi`, `loro non hanno`/`eßi non hanno`, `e' si
rimangono`/`eßi rimangono`, `stando ambigui`/`stando dubbiosi`,
`rispettivi`/`con rispetto`. The rest is the 1554's spelling profile
(`Prencipe`, `eſſercito`, `sendo`/`eſſendo`, `Rep.`, tildes).

## FG-CONS.1.30 — Canestrini, p. 48

92 words (A and B). Both passes find the heading CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO XXX. near the head of p. 48 under the running head, with nothing
of an earlier reply on the page; Canestrini's three-line rubric with a
hanging indent; one indented paragraph from `Io laudo` to `di questi
sinistri.`; one note of Canestrini's keyed to `Discorso` (`Cioè che un
principe, per fuggire la necessità ... quelli che sono virtuosi.`), a
paraphrase of Machiavelli's opening sentence; then CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO XXXII on the same page. There is no reply on XXXI.
`paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). A marks 11 body letters doubtful, B
12: barless e, u broken into n or a, s blotted into a.

**Word-level splits.** None. The 107 tokens are identical after preferred
readings. Pass B gives `comunemente` as the alternative to its preferred
`communemente`; one word, and the 1933 edition prints `communemente`.

**Doubtful to both, the alternatives different words.**

- Rubric, `un principe ⟨a|o⟩ una repubblica`: both readers see an a and
  offer o. `A` does not construe in the disjunction; the 1933 edition
  prints `o`, as does Machiavelli's rubric in both editions. Read `o`, a
  reading at a letter the scan breaks, as D111: **D322**.
- `⟨E|È⟩ ne vediamo`: a mark over the capital. `È` does not construe; the
  1933 edition prints `E`. Read `E`, as D307: **D323**.

The other doubts common to both (`tntti`/`tatti`, `snoi`/`sooi`, `lc`,
`capitam`, `aia`, `frntto`/`fratto`, `ano`, `rarisaime`, `Consideaazione`,
`ingratitudina`, `qûel`, `assere`) have no word for an alternative.

**Against the 1933 edition.** No word-level difference at a letter the
scan breaks. Whole-word differences: `espedizioni`/`espedizione`
(Palmarocchi's -e plural), `il ricordo`/`el ricordo`, `reputazione`/
`riputazione`, `tutto dì`/`tuttodí`: his orthographic profile. None bears
on sense.
