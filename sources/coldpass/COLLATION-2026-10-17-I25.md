# Pair I.25 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together.
The NM sample page was chosen after pass A came back and given to a fifth
reader alone. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini page
at 1200 ppi (native 600) in halves enlarged 1.6x, with quarter-page crops
enlarged 2.2x; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in thirds. The collator read the
1933 page against the passes, and checked the 1554 readings cited below on
the leaf images.

## NM-DISC.1.25 — 1824, pp. 100–101

333 words (pass A: p. 100 89, p. 101 244; elided forms counted apart).
Pass A finds CAPITOLO XXV on p. 100 after line 13, the last line of XXIV
(`ch'egli prima con tanta sua gloria aveva salvato.`), the three-line italic
rubric `Chi vuole riformare uno Stato antico in una Città libera, ritenga
almeno l' ombra de' modi antichi.`, a large initial C, one printed block
running on across the page turn without an indent, no notes, and the end at
p. 101 line 29 `come nel seguente capitolo si dirà.`, the rest of the page
blank; CAPITOLO XXVI opens p. 102.

**The sample (D199, D203(c)).** Pass A marked 9 loci: 5 on p. 100, 4 on
p. 101. P. 100 carries 89 words of the chapter (27%, at least a fifth), and
has the most pass-A doubts. The collator chose p. 100 and recorded the
choice before giving it out. Pass B read only that page.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The body tokens of p. 100
are identical between the passes after Class A. Pass A's four doubts there
are worn e's (`vuolc`, `mantcnere`, `ritenerc`, `chc`), which pass B reads
without doubt; both read the raised stop after `sono` as a full stop. Both
read `almanco` in the body against `almeno` in the rubric. The full pass B is
not triggered, and the sample rule stands for the next chapter.

**Outside the sample (D203(a)).** Pass A's four doubts on p. 101 are
`Capo 'di`, `mancamenio`, `in'una`, `ritcngano`: none has a word for its
alternative. Nothing to raise.

**Against the 1554 (D203(b)).** At letters the scans break, the word diff
finds only spellings of one word: `crearono`/`creorono` and
`chiamarono`/`chiamorono` (the old Florentine perfect; the Giglio reader
doubted the second), as `confidenza`/`confidanza` in I.24. Not escalated.

**Raised although no rule requires it.** .03 `ritengano più dell'antico sia
possibile` (p. 101): pass A reads no `che`, without doubt, outside the
sample; the 1554 prints `che sia possibile` (checked on fol. 40v). A whole
word at a sense-bearing point, resting on one reader. It construes as an
elliptical comparative: kept, D38, **D270**.

## Comparison witnesses

**Witness independence (SCOPE §7).** At the two split loci pass A sides with
Palmarocchi, pass B with the printed shape; in each the other form is not a
word. The decisive test: neither pass carries a Palmarocchi word-reading
where Canestrini differs. Both have `canclusione`, `verbi gratia`, plain `e
Romani`, `Sacrificulo`, `governo,`, `religione;`, against the 1933
`conclusione`, `verbigrazia`, `e' romani`, `sacrificulo`, `governo;`,
`religione,`. The passes read Canestrini.

**FG, 1933 edition.** Word differences from Canestrini: `conclusione`
(D267). The rest is Palmarocchi's orthographic profile (`verbigrazia`, lower
case `romani`, `sacrificulo`, `sí`, `potestá`) and his pointing. His rubric
reads `stato anticato`, against Canestrini's and the 1824's `antico`;
heading apparatus, recorded only here.

**NM, 1554 Giglio.** A third reader transcribed fol. 40v–41r cold
(`NM-DISC.1.25-1554.md`); the transcript was collated by word against the
established 1824 text, and the readings below were checked on the leaves by
the collator. The differences that bear on the English:

- .03 the 1554 has no `o per via di Repubblica, o di Regno; ma quello che
  vuol fare una potestà assoluta,`: an eye-skip from one `assoluta` to the
  next, which leaves the renewal of everything prescribed to whoever would
  order an absolute power, and ends `si dira,`.
- .01 `diuersi` for `alieni`.
- .02 `al Sacerdote`, without `sommo`.
- .03 `che sia possibile` (D270).
- .01 `al manco`, as the 1824 `almanco`; both witnesses print `almeno` in
  the rubric.
