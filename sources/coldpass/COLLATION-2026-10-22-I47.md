# Pair I.47 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together,
each reader with its own image directory and its own output folder. Under
D302 the NM sample page waited for pass A and the Giglio read, was chosen by
the density of word-level 1554 differences, and went to a fifth reader alone
with line crops of every word-level 1554 difference outside it. The 1824
pages went at 350 ppi, whole and in thirds; the Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi
(native 600), whole and in halves; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi, whole and
in thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages against the passes and checked
the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf images.

**The overwrite, again, and its cause (D370).** The first NM pass-A file
came back with its own front matter and loci and its body replaced by the
Giglio transcription, as at I.39. Separate output folders had not prevented
it, because they were not the channel: every subagent inherits the session's
scratchpad directory, and both readers drafted their body there under the
same name, `body.txt`. The Giglio reader wrote it at 14:40:50; pass A
assembled its output from it three seconds later. Both files were discarded
and both readers rerun, each confined to its own work folder and forbidden
the scratchpad and `/tmp`. The FG readers had finished before the collision
and their passes differ as independent readings do; they stand. The I.39
failure was very likely the same mechanism.

`fazione` occurs in neither unit.
