# Pair I.16 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together,
before the collator had seen any page. The NM sample page was chosen after
pass A came back and given to a fifth reader alone. The 1824 pages went at
350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi (native 600) in halves,
with enlarged quarter-page crops; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in thirds.
A cold read of the 1933 pages by a subagent was attempted and did not
complete; the collator read the 1933 pages against the passes instead.

## NM-DISC.1.16 — 1824, pp. 75–80

1,186 words (pass A). Pass A finds CAPITOLO XVI on p. 75 after line 7, the
last line of XV, the three-line italic rubric `Un Popolo uso a vivere sotto
un Principe, se per qualche accidente diventa libero, con difficultà mantiene
la libertà.`, a drop capital Q, one printed block, no notes, and the end at
p. 80 line 8 `mostreremo.`, with CAPITOLO XVII following.

**The sample (D199, D203(c)).** Pass A marked two loci in the whole chapter:
one on p. 77, one on p. 80. P. 80 carries 58 words of the chapter, under a
fifth; p. 77 carries 253 (21%). The collator chose p. 77 and recorded the
choice before giving it out. Pass B read only that page.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The 253 word tokens of the
page, and the pointing, are identical between the passes after Class A. Both
readers doubt the same faint mark after `Principi` and take the comma. Pass
B's two other marks are apostrophe spacing. The full pass B is not
triggered, and the sample rule stands for the next chapter.

**Outside the sample (D203(a)).** Pass A's one other doubt is p. 80 `quando
⟨ei|ci⟩ ricuperò`, where the alternatives are different words. To David with
the 1554 reading `quando ei recupero`: `ei`, **D217**.

**Against the 1554 (D203(b)).** Word-level differences at letters the scan
