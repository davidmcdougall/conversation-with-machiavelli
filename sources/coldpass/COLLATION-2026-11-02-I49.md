# Pair I.49 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together,
each reader confined to its own image, work and output folders and forbidden
the session scratchpad and `/tmp`, the channel of the I.39 and I.47
overwrites (D370); no output was overwritten this time. Under D302 the NM
sample page waited for pass A and the Giglio read, was chosen by the density
of word-level 1554 differences, and went to a fifth reader alone with line
crops of every word-level 1554 difference outside it. The 1824 pages went at

## NM-DISC.1.49 — 1824, pp. 165–168

810 words (pass A: p. 165 161, p. 166 253, p. 167 242, p. 168 154). Pass A
finds CAPITOLO XLIX at the head of p. 165, below the running head (the end
of XLVIII is on p. 164, outside the extract), a five-line italic rubric, a
drop initial Q (`Quanto sia difficile`), one printed block with no notes,
and the end at p. 168 body line 18 `che le non si possano riordinare mai.`,
with CAPITOLO L following on the same page. No signature. Pass A marks 7
loci, all specks, accents or type damage with no word for an alternative
(`ebbero`, `creazione`, `trovar`, `Ma`, `cittadino`, `che`, the rubric's
`una`), so D203(a) raises nothing.

**The sample (D302).** Eligible pages, at least a fifth of the chapter
(162 words): p. 166 (253) and p. 167 (242); p. 165 (161) and p. 168 (154)
fall just under. Word-level 1554 differences after spelling normalisation:
p. 165 one (`a tenere`/`tenere`), p. 166 two (`il principio libero`/`il
principio loro libero`, `rotto`/`retto`), p. 167 two (`pericoloso`/
`pericolosa`, `collocata`/`posta`), p. 168 one (`de' pochi`/`di pochi`). No
word repeats. Per word: p. 167 0.0083, p. 166 0.0079: **p. 167**, recorded
before it was given out.

With the page went three crops of three or four lines each, carrying every
word-level 1554 difference outside it (p. 165 ll. 11-14, p. 166 ll. 11-14,
p. 168 ll. 1-3), in shuffled order and without saying which word.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The 265 tokens of p. 167
are identical between the passes after Class A but for `Ma`/`Mà`, a mark
both readers doubted and neither takes for an accent on a different word.
Every crop line matches pass A, `rotto` included.

**Against the 1554 (to David).**

  (e/o, D203(b)). Read by pass A and the crop reader without doubt; seen by

  Recommendation: emend `r[e]tto` under D68 as extended by D126, with a

- p. 167 `fusse pericoloso` / `pericolosa` (o/a, D203(b)), read by both
  readers without doubt. The 1824 agrees with `il differire`, the 1554 with
  `la esecuzione`. Recommendation: kept, D38, **D380**.

Recorded as variant notes without ruling, each read by two readers: `a`
before `tenere`, `loro` before `libero`, `collocata`/`posta`, `de' pochi`/
`di pochi`. The rest is form and spelling (see the comparison record, D004).

## FG-CONS.1.49 — Canestrini, p. 54

169 words (A 169, B 169). Both passes find the heading CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO XLIX at the head of p. 54, below the running head; a three-line
rubric; one indented paragraph, `E questo Discorso` (body l. 1) to `benchè
lo credo` (l. 17); then CONSIDERAZIONE SUL CAPITOLO LVIII on the same page.
Canestrini's note (1) at the foot of p. 54 is keyed to `populo` in the
LVIII reply and is not carried. `paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). A
marks 17 loci, B 17.

**Body: one word split.** The tokens are identical after preferred readings
but for `sì` (A) / `si` (B), each giving the other as alternative, and the
point after `solo`. Only the adverb construes; the 1933 edition reads `sí`.
Recommendation: `sì`, **D382**.

**Printed non-words read by both.** `non lasciare ⟨cha|che⟩` (l. 6) and
`Fu anche ⟨arrore|errore⟩` (l. 9): both readers prefer the printed `a` and
give the e-form as the alternative; the 1933 edition reads `che`, `errore`.
Recommendation: emend `ch[e]`, `[e]rrore` under D68, as D267, **D381**. Not
adjudicated on the page image.

**Doubtful to both, the alternatives words.**

- `quello che ⟨io|lo⟩` (A) / `⟨io|to⟩` (B) (l. 1): both prefer `io`; the 1933
  edition reads `io`.
- rubric `⟨ne|nè⟩ hanno`: both prefer `ne`; the 1933 edition and the 1824
  rubric read `ne`.
- `o che ⟨e|è⟩` (A) / `⟨e|i⟩` (B) `seguenti` (l. 15): both prefer `e`, the
  plural article; the 1933 edition reads `e'`.
- `⟨il|el⟩ che` (l. 16): both prefer `il`; the 1933 edition reads `il`.
- `benchè ⟨lo|io⟩ credo` (l. 17): both prefer `lo`; the 1933 edition reads
  `lo`.

Recommendation: read with the 1933 edition, **D383**.

Single doubts (A `de'`, `dua`; B `feciono`, `leggi`, `quelli`, `vi era`) are
read alike by the other reader and by the 1933 edition.
