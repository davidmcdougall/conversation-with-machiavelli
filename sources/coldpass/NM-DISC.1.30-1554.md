# Reader's notes
- Foliation: page 2 carries "46" at top right beside running head "PRIMO" (recto). Pages 1 and 3 carry "LIBRO" only (versos, unnumbered). Running heads read across the opening LIBRO | PRIMO. Page 1 therefore = fol. 45v, page 3 = fol. 46v (inference, not printed).
- No signature mark and no catchword visible on any of the three pages (bottom of 45v ends on "le fer-" with no catchword below; bottom of 46r has only stray ink/ornament specks below "Romani.").
- No marginalia or printed side-notes on any page. Faint show-through from the other side of the leaf on all pages.
- Heading set in roman capitals (first line) then italic; "Cap. X X X." spaced out. Body in italic with roman capitals for some initials (Prencipe, Romani, Repub., Roma, Dittatura, Signore).
- Opening: woodcut/large initial V (three lines deep), followed by small capital N ("VN Prencipe").
- Line-end hyphens: printed as a small raised dot or short double stroke, transcribed "-". Several word breaks carry no visible hyphen and are transcribed as printed: ne / gligenza, ca / ſa, mede / ſimi, quel / li. Stray end-of-line specks (after "che" 45v ll.10 and 15, "tenga", "nuoue") are not transcribed.
- Spacing kept approximately: "ſono(eſſendo", "ma(come", "diſſe)gli" printed without space; "l'altre ." has a space before the point.
- Mid-line dots in 45v l.22 ("inſolente·o·ambitioſo") look like raised spacing material, not punctuation; not transcribed.
