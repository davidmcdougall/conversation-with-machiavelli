# Pair I.60 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together,
each reader confined to its own image, work and output folders outside the
session scratchpad and forbidden it and `/tmp` (D370). NM pass B followed
once the sample page had been chosen, in folders of its own. No output was
overwritten; each body matches its own witness. The 1824 pages went at
350 ppi, whole and in thirds; the Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi (native
600), whole and in halves; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi, whole and in
thirds. The collator read the 1933 page against the passes and saw the
three 1824 crops before they were given out.

`fazione` occurs in neither unit.

## NM-DISC.1.60 — 1824, pp. 202–203

360 words (pass A: p. 202 177, p. 203 183). Pass A finds CAPITOLO LX on p.
202 after the four-line close of LIX (`fidare più di lui che del
Principe.`), a two-line italic rubric, a drop initial E (`E' si vede`), one
printed block with no notes, one Latin phrase in italic (`erat praemium
virtutis non sanguinis`), and the end at p. 203 l. 22 `giovanissimi.`,
followed by FINE DEL LIBRO PRIMO. The third extract page is blank. Pass A
marks 5 loci, all marks or damaged sorts in the same word.

**The sample (D302).** Both pages are eligible (each over a fifth of 360).
Word-level 1554 differences after spelling normalisation: p. 202 three
(`nella`/`nelle`, `fece`/`fece in`, `il Consolato`/`Consolato`), 3/177; p.
203 five (`si nutrì`/`nutri`, `non ha a fare`/`ha a fare`,
`avendolo`/`hauendouelo`, `la Città`/`ella citta`, `di Pompeo`/`& di
Pompeio`), 5/183. Sample page **p. 203**, recorded before it was given out.
Pass B read it whole, with crops of p. 202 ll. 3, 15–16 and 20 (the lines
holding the three p. 202 differences), not told which word.

**Result: clean.** Pass B's p. 203 is identical to pass A's, 182 tokens to
182 (183 words by the count's apostrophe rule); the crops read `nella
Plebe`, `che fece / Roma`, `avere il Consolato`, as pass A. Pass B's two
doubts are a speck (`V.alerio`) and a faint `e` at a crop edge.

**Against the 1554 (to David).**

- D203(b), at e/a, read by both 1824 readers without doubt: `nella Plebe` /
  `nelle plebe`. The 1824 agrees with the singular.
- Whole words: `che fece Roma` / `che fece in Roma`; `avere il Consolato` /
  `hauere Consolato`; `si nutrì` / `nutri`; `non ha a fare questa
  distinzione` / `ha a fare`; `avendolo ad eleggere` / `hauendouelo`. The
  1554's dropped `non` reverses the sense: a city that would do what Rome
  did must, in the 1554, make the distinction of blood the 1824 says it
  cannot make. The 1824 construes at each.
- Recommendation: kept, D38 and D203(b), with a translator's note on `non
  ha a fare`. **D409.**

Recorded as variant notes without ruling: `la fusse` / `ella fusse`; `la
Città` / `ella citta`; `e di Pompeo` / `& di Pompeio`; the numeral `XXIII.`
for `ventitré`. The rest is form and spelling (the comparison record, D004).

## FG-CONS.1.60 — Canestrini, pp. 59–60

170 words (A 110 + 60, B 110 + 60). Both passes find CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO LX on p. 59 after the end of the LVIII reply (`in uno populo che
in uno principe`); a two-line rubric with no stop; one indented paragraph;
the end at p. 60 l. 6 `non essendo trattate nel Discorso`, no stop visible
to either; CONSIDERAZIONE SUL PROEMIO DEL LIBRO II follows. Canestrini's
note (1) on the lacuna (`Lacuna nel testo. Forse l'Autore accenna al
trattato De Legibus, libro III ...`) is his commentary and is not carried
as text; its conjecture is reported, as his, in the translator's note.
`paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). A marks 12 loci in the body, B 9.

**Body: no word split.** The 170 tokens are identical after Class A.

**The rubric.** `qualunqua` (A, `qualunque` its alternative) / `qualunque`
(B). A form of one word; the 1933 edition, the 1824 rubric and the 1554
heading (`qualunche`) have the -e form; the a-shape is the scan's barless
e. Recommendation: `qualunque`, **D406**.

**The lacuna.** Both readers see a row of points after `Cicerone nel` with
Canestrini's note call (A four, B three, each doubting the count); the
1933 edition prints five. Both witnesses leave the work unnamed. The
edition's first. Recommendation: `. . .` in the Italian and the English,
unbracketed (brackets are the edition's supplies, D53, D68); a translator's
note giving the gap, Canestrini's conjecture as his, and the editor's
identification, **D407**.

**Against the 1933 edition (to David).** `bisogna dire, e così con verità,
che` / `e così è con verità`; both read `così con` without doubt.
Canestrini construes as an ellipsis. Recommendation: kept, D38, "[it is]"
supplied in the English, **D408**.

Doubtful to both, the alternative a non-word, read as the word and not
raised: `una`/`nna`, `tra`/`tia`, `o`/`n no`, `principii`/`principij`
