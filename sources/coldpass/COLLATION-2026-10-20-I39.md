# Pair I.39 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together.
Under D302 the NM sample page waited for pass A and the Giglio read, was
chosen by the density of word-level 1554 differences, and went to a fifth
reader alone with line crops of every word-level 1554 difference outside
it. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini pages at 1200
ppi (native 600), whole and in halves; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in
thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages against the passes, and checked
the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf images.

**An isolation failure, caught and rerun.** NM pass A and FG pass A were
given one output folder, and the Giglio reader another. The first NM pass-A
file came back with its front matter and loci intact and its body replaced
by the Giglio transcription: one reader's output had been written over
another's. Neither file could be trusted to be cold. Both were discarded
and not used, and NM pass A and the Giglio read were rerun, each with
its own image directory and its own output folder. The FG pass-A file in
the shared folder was intact and contains only Canestrini text; it stands.
The discarded Giglio read and the rerun agree on every word. From here on
every reader writes to a folder no other reader is given.

D317 is closed by D341; `fazione` occurs in neither unit.
