# Pair I.11 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together,
before the collator had seen any page. The NM sample page was chosen after
pass A came back and given to a fifth reader alone. The 1824 pages went at
350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi (native 600) in halves,
with enlarged quarter-page crops; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in thirds.

## NM-DISC.1.11 — 1824, pp. 58–62

1,076 words (pass A). Pass A finds CAPITOLO XI on p. 58 below the last three
lines of X, the italic rubric `Della Religione de' Romani.`, a two-line drop
capital A, one printed block, no notes, Dante's tercet set as three indented
italic lines on p. 61, and the end at p. 62 l. 20 `con un medesimo ordine.`,
with CAPITOLO XII following.

**The sample (D199).** The collator chose p. 61: 235 words, 22% of the
chapter, and the page holding most of pass A's doubts (5 of 18), the Dante
tercet and the chapter's thesis. The criterion was stated before the page was
given out (D203). Pass B read only that page.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The 265 tokens of the page
are identical between the passes after Class A, and identical before it. Both
readers doubt the same specks (`marmo` with a broken o, a blot in `quali`, a
mark over `sono`, faint `te` in `salute`) and read the same letters at each.
At `corta vita, conviene` pass A reads a malformed comma ⟨,⟩, pass B ⟨,|;⟩;
both take the comma first, and it is kept. The full pass B is not triggered,
and the sample rule stands for the next chapter.

**Outside the sample.** Pass A's eighteen doubts are specks and damaged sorts,
except one: p. 59 `come ⟨sì|si⟩ vede`, a grave or a dot over the i, where the
alternatives are different words. Under D203 it went to David with the 1554
reading (`come si uede`): `si`, **D202**.

## FG-CONS.1.11 — Canestrini, pp. 26–27

About 340 words (A 339, B 345; they count elisions differently). Both passes
find the heading CONSIDERAZIONE SUL CAPITOLO XI. on p. 26 after `che abbia
fatto.`, Canestrini's rubric `Della religione de' Romani` without a stop, one
indented paragraph, no notes belonging to the reply (the note on p. 27 is
keyed to the reply on XII), and the end at p. 27 l. 21 `chi la ordinassi con
la religione.`: `paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). A marks 31 loci
doubtful, B 30, nearly all broken u (read as n), barless e (read as a or o)
and damaged s.

**After Class A the passes differ in no word.** The only token differences
are A's inline hyphens at line ends and the space after an elided apostrophe.

## Comparison witnesses

**Witness independence (SCOPE §7).** The FG passes split at no word, so there
is no split set to check; neither pass carries a Palmarocchi word-reading
(both have `o`, `armi`, `Romolo`, `mettere le armi`, `animi`, `prudenza`
against the 1933 `e`, `arme`, `Romulo`, `mettervi le arme`, `uomini`,
`prudenzia`).

**FG, 1933 edition.** Readings that differ in word from Canestrini, for the
variant notes: `e l'arme e la religione` (D200); `uomini` for `animi`;
`mettervi le arme` for `mettere le armi`; `maggiore` for `maggiori`;
`s'avessi` for `si avessi`; `buone legge`, `delle arte`; `prudenzia`. The
rest is Palmarocchi's orthographic profile (`el`, `popolo`, `Di poi`,
`ed ancora`, `ed uso`, `adunche`) and his pointing, and is not enumerated.

**NM, 1554 Giglio.** A third reader transcribed fol. 21v–23v cold
(`NM-DISC.1.11-1554.md`); the transcript was collated by word against the
established 1824 text, spelling normalised (u/v, h, -ti-/-zi-, doubled
consonants, `&`). The readings cited below were checked on the leaf images
by the collator, except `nudritura`, `ordinò` (.01), `diedero` (.05), `d'esse`,
`dependeno ... alla` (.06), `li credevano` and `d'altrui` (.07), which rest
on the transcript. The differences that bear on the English:

- .01 `la nudritura` for `la educazione`; `ordinò` for `costituì`; `Numma`
  throughout.
- .02 `come si uede` (D202); `sbigottiti della patria` for `sbigottiti e
  paurosi`; `Lucio Mallio, che fu dipoi chiamato Torquato`, without `padre di
  Tito Manlio`; `l'amore della patria, le leggi` without `e`; `di molte|molti
  de Romani`.
- .03 `fusse obligata` without `più`; `credo piu tosto` without `che`;
  `a comandare a esserciti`.
- .04 `finse di havere domestichezza con una Ninfa, laquale lo informava` for
  `simulò di avere congresso ..., la quale lo consigliava`; `ad altrui`.
- .05 `diedero` for `dettono`.
- .06 `avenimetti` (for `avvenimenti`) for `successi`; `della Republica` and
  `di quella` for `delle Repubbliche` and `di quello`; `d'esse`;
  `dependeno solo alla virtù`; Dante `Quei`; no `avere` before `un
  Prencipe`; `ella si mantega`.
- .07 `o una opinione`; `che si presumono`; `li credevano`; `d'altrui`.
