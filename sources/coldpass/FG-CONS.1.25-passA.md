# Body

La ⟨canclusione|conclusione⟩ del Discorso è più ⟨necessaria|necessarìa⟩ a chi non muta spezie di governo, ma lo riforma, verbi gratia a chi vuole introdurre nuovi ordini in ⟨una|ana⟩ città libera, che ⟨a|n⟩ chi muta spezie di governo, perchè se di ⟨uno|ano⟩ regno io introduco una libertà, come feciono e Romani, ⟨essendo|esaando⟩ già nella opinione degli uomini che quello vivere non sia ⟨buono|baono⟩, non accade conservare ⟨sì|si⟩ esattamente gli ordini antichi. # Reader's notes

- Heading, stop after XXV: only a faint grey trace after the V; the following heading (XXVI) has a clear full stop, so a stop has probably dropped out of the scan.
- Rubric, "antico": the t has lost its crossbar and looks like b/l ("anbco"); same effect in "antichi" (looks "anfichi"), which I read as antichi with confidence.
- Rubric, final stop after "antichi": faint grey trace only.
- Body l.1 "canclusione": the second letter shows as a complete a (bowl and stem), not a broken o. Could be a printer's error or a filled o; I give the reading the page shows first.
- Body l.1 "necessaria": the r-i-a group is blotched; the i looks as if it carries a mark (necessarìa). Probably damage, not an accent.
- Body l.3 "ana città": the first letter looks like a/n; I read it as u broken up (una).
- Body l.3, last word of the line, "che n": a single isolated n-shape after a wide space before "chi muta". "che n chi" makes no sense; I read it as a broken a (che a chi). The shape on the page is n.
- Body l.4 "di ano regno": the first letter shows as an n/a-shape; I read u (uno), a common breakage of u.
- Body l.5 "esaando": the third and fourth letters both look like a. The warned s→a and e→a breakages give "essendo"; the page literally shows "esaando".
- Body l.6 "buono": the u is partly filled and looks a-like ("baono"); probably u.
- Body l.7 "sì esattamente": the first letter looks like a, but there is a clear grave accent over the i: sì.
- Body l.9 "nell uno": the space after "nell" carries only a faint grey mark at apostrophe height; apostrophe probably there but broken. Also "momento ," is printed with a space before the comma (typographic spacing; not counted as a doubt).
- Body l.12 "si armassino": no accent visible over the i; there is a faint grey speck above it. Read si, alternative sì.
- Body l.12 "e consuli": a small mark at the upper right of the e could be a grave accent (è) or a speck; read e (article, = "i").
- Body l.13 final stop after "potestà": only a faint grey trace; the stop is likely there but lost.
- Line-end hyphenations joined: in-|troduco, supersti-|zione, nel-|l' altro (given as "nell' altro", with the printed space after the apostrophe kept), pote-|stà.
