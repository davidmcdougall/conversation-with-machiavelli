# Heading

CAPITOLO XXVIII

# Rubric

*Per qual cagione i Romani furono meno ingrati*
*ai loro cittadini che gli Ateniesi.*

# Reader's notes

- The print is soft. Throughout, 'e' often looks like 'c' (for example 'Qualunquc', 'Atenc', 'sc', 'cd', 'essere'). Where the word is clear I have read 'e' silently. I have marked only the loci where the letterform is truly ambiguous.
- p. 105 l. 1: 'Qualunque' opens with a large initial Q. The last letter of the word looks like 'c'.
- p. 105 l. 1: in 'fatte', the second t is faint and could be read as 'i'.
- p. 105 ll. 5-6: there is a small slanting stroke between 'altra' and 'Repubblica', set below the line and above 'parlando' on the next line. It looks like a stray mark, not an accent, and I have not transcribed it.
- p. 105: '<' marks in the left margin of the image are scanning or gutter artefacts.
- p. 106 l. 1: the 'che' after 'civiltà,' is blurred and looks like 'ehc'. The reading 'che' is preferred.
- p. 106 l. 12: the page prints 'coniettura' with a plain i, not 'conjettura'. Note that the edition does use j elsewhere ('varj', 'Tarquinj').
- p. 106 l. 21: 'arebbe' is printed as a single word with one r ('arebbe', not 'avrebbe'). Transcribed as printed.
- p. 106 l. 7: there is a small dot under the 'c' of 'che' ('accidenti che'), probably an ink speck.
- p. 106: the lower part of the page is blank apart from show-through of lines from the verso.
- The page numbers 105 and 106 use old-style figures. Running heads: 'LIBRO PRIMO' on recto pages, 'DISCORSI' on verso pages.
