# Missing stops

None in the body. At both places where the sense needs a period (p. 26 l. 13 "religione Fu", p. 27 l. 21 the final word) a faint trace of a stop is visible, so they are given as ⟨.⟩ and counted as uncertain punctuation (loci 29-30). The rubric has no final stop, which matches the unstopped Cap. XII rubric, so it is not counted.
