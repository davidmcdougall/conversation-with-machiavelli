# Pair I.26 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together.
The NM sample page was chosen after pass A came back and given to a fifth
reader alone. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini pages
at 1200 ppi in halves enlarged 1.6x, with quarter-page crops enlarged 2.2x;
the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages
against the passes, and checked the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf
images.

## NM-DISC.1.26 — 1824, pp. 102–103

268 words (pass A: p. 102 204, p. 103 64; elided forms counted apart). Pass
A finds CAPITOLO XXVI at the head of p. 102 under the running head, the
italic rubric `Un Principe nuovo in una Città o Provincia presa da lui,
debbe fare ogni cosa nuova.`, a large initial Q, one printed block running
on across the page turn inside `qua-|lunque`, no notes, and the end at p.
103 line 9 `per esempio si mostrerà.`; CAPITOLO XXVII and its rubric follow
on the same page. The Luke quotation is printed in italic.

**The sample (D199, D203(c)).** Pass A marked 19 loci: 3 on p. 102, 16 on
p. 103. P. 103 carries 64 words of the chapter (24%, at least a fifth), and
has the most pass-A doubts. The collator chose p. 103 and recorded the
choice before giving it out. Pass B read only that page.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The body tokens of p. 103
are identical between the passes after Class A (76 tokens each, punctuation
included). Pass A's sixteen doubts there are worn e's printing as c and u/n
(`fuggirc`, `vivcrc`, `nomini`, `escmpio`), which pass B reads without
doubt or marks the same way; pass B's own doubts are `piuttosto`/`piultosto`,
`tutti`/`tulti`, a speck before `dannosissime`, and spaces before stops.
The full pass B is not triggered, and the sample rule stands for the next
chapter.

**Outside the sample (D203(a)).** Pass A's three doubts on p. 102 are
`dimisit`/`climisit`, `solamente`/`solamenle`, `debbegli`/`debbcgli`: none
has a word for its alternative. Nothing to raise.

**Against the 1554 (D203(b)).** At letters the scans break the word diff
finds two differences of word on p. 102, both read by pass A without doubt:
.01 `con nuova autorità`, 1554 `nuoue auttorita` (a/e), and .02 `oltra di
questo nuove Città`, 1554 `di queste` (o/e). Both checked on fol. 41r. Both
1824 readings construe: kept, D38, **D281**. The rest are spellings of one
word (`fussero`/`fusseno`).

**Raised although no rule requires it.** .03 `come nel presente capitolo per
esempio si mostrerà` (p. 103, the sample, both readers without doubt): the
1554 prints `seguente` (checked on fol. 41v). The example the clause promises
is in I.27, whose rubric repeats the clause, so the 1554 reading is the apt
one; but `presente` is Italian and construes. Kept, D38, **D282**, with a
variant note and a translator's note.

## Comparison witnesses

**Witness independence (SCOPE §7).** At the one word split pass A keeps the
printed shape, pass B the 1933 form, which A gives as alternative. Neither
pass carries a Palmarocchi word-reading where Canestrini differs: both have
`il principato`, `il trattarli`, `il principe`, `mutazioni`, `armi proprie`,
`guadagnarla`, `necessarii remedii`, `estraordinarii`, `comunemente`, an
unbracketed `non`, against the 1933 `el principato`, `el trattarli`, `el
principe`, `mutazione`, `arme proprie`, `guadagnarli`, `necessari remedi`,
`estraordinari`, `communemente`, `[non]`. The passes read Canestrini.

**FG, 1933 edition.** Word differences from Canestrini: `dello` (D279),
`guadagnarli`, the bracketed `[non]`, `delle mutazione`; the rest is
Palmarocchi's orthographic profile (`el`, `-i` for `-ii`, `arme`,
`communemente`, `sí`) and his pointing (`forti,`, `superiore,`). His rubric
reads `Uno principe nuovo`, bracketed as his.

**NM, 1554 Giglio.** A third reader transcribed fol. 41r–41v cold
(`NM-DISC.1.26-1554.md`); the transcript was collated by word against the
established 1824 text, and the readings below were checked on the leaves by
the collator. The differences that bear on the English:

- .03 `seguente capitolo` for `presente capitolo` (D282).
- .02 `disfare delle fatte`, "unmake some of those made", for `delle
  vecchie`, "some of the old".
- .02 `oltra di queste` for `oltra di questo` (D281).
- .02 `tramutò`, perfect, for the imperfect `tramutava`.
- .01 `nuoue auttorita`, plural, for `nuova autorità` (D281).
- .01 the Latin flagged by marginal inverted commas, `Re. Qui esurientes`.
