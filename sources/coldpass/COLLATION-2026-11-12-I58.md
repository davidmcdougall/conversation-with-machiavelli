# Pair I.58 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together,
each reader confined to its own image, work and output folders outside the
session scratchpad and forbidden it and `/tmp` (D370). NM pass B followed once
the sample page had been sought and none found, in folders of its own. No
output was overwritten; each body matches its own witness. The 1824 pages
went at 350 ppi, whole and in thirds; the Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi
(native 600), whole and in halves; the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi, whole and in
thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages against the passes and saw the
cited Canestrini and 1824 loci on the page images.

`fazione` occurs in neither unit.

## NM-DISC.1.58 — 1824, pp. 192–199

1,775 words (A and B alike: p. 192 187, p. 193 248, p. 194 256, p. 195 256,
p. 196 270, p. 197 267, p. 198 263, p. 199 28). Both passes find CAPITOLO
LVIII at the head of p. 192, below the running head, a two-line italic
rubric, a drop initial N (`Nessuna cosa`), one printed block with no notes,
two Livy quotations in italic, and the end at p. 199 body line 4 `o di
quelle fatte con un Principe.`, with CAPITOLO LIX following on the same
page. The signature `13` at the foot of p. 193 is not body. Pass A marks 5
loci, pass B 7, all marks or damaged sorts but one (below).

**The sample (D302): none possible (D391).** An eligible page must hold a
fifth of the chapter, 355 words. The fullest, p. 196, holds 270 (15%). As at
I.40 (D352), pass B read the whole chapter, the rule's ordinary course before
D199 and the stricter one; no crops were needed. For the record, the
word-level 1554 differences by page, after spelling normalisation, are p. 192
3, p. 193 5, p. 194 3, p. 195 5, p. 196 1, p. 197 3, p. 198 2 (counting forms
of one word once), p. 199 0.

**Result: one split, no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The 1,775
tokens are identical after Class A, and so is the pointing, but for p. 197
`hann⟨o|e⟩ durato assai gli Stati delle Repubbliche`: A reads `hanno` (a worn
sort, `hannc` its alternative), B reads the letter as e, `hanne`, not a word.
The collator saw the page: the sort is open on the right, a broken o or c,
not a closed e. The 1554 reads `hanno`. Recommendation: read `hanno`, with a
variant note, **D392**.

**Against the 1554 (to David).**

- D203(b), at o/a, each read by both passes without doubt: `dell'altro
  sciolto` / `dell'altra`; `ei temono` twice / `temano`; `pajano` /
  `paiono`. The 1824 construes at each. Recommendation: kept, D38, **D393**.
- Sense-bearing whole words, each read by both passes without doubt:
  `conchiudo adunque contro alla comune opinione` / `oltre alla`;
  `malattia` twice / `malitia`; `perché e' sono e sono stati assai Principi` /
  `perche de cattiui sono`; `chi considererà` / `considera`; `si troverà in
  lei` / `si truoui`. The 1824 construes at each. Recommendation: kept, D38,
  with translator's notes on `contro` and `malattia`, **D394**.

Recorded as variant notes without ruling: the 1554's Italian renderings of
both Livy quotations (`Cioè ...`), which the 1824 does not print; `tutti gli
uomini`, `lo desiderava`, `la forza`, `l'è sentenza`, `essi` for `loro` and
for `egli`, `massimamente`, `de'`/`di`. The rest is form and spelling (the
comparison record, D006).

## FG-CONS.1.58 — Canestrini, pp. 54–59

973 words (A 973, B 973). Both passes find the heading CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO LVIII on p. 54 after the end of the XLIX reply; a one-line rubric
with no stop; three indented paragraphs (`Difficile impresa`, p. 54; `Gli
esempli`, p. 56 l. 17; `Di poi`, p. 57 l. 14); the end at p. 59 `in uno
populo che in uno principe`, no stop visible to either; CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO LX follows. Canestrini's three notes (keyed to `populo`,
`prudenti.`, `vogliono,`) are his commentary and are not carried.
`paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). A marks 56 loci in the body and 2 in
the rubric, B 41 and 2, most of them lost or faint points.

**Body: two word splits; the rubric one.**

- Rubric `più ⟨savia|savio⟩`: A `savia`, B `savio`, each giving the other.
  Only `savia` agrees with `moltitudine`; the 1933 edition and the 1824
  rubric read `savia`. Recommendation: `savia`, **D395**.
- p. 56 l. 26 `né lo ⟨esemplo|esamplo⟩`: A prefers `esemplo` but calls the
  letter a clear a; B reads `esamplo`. Not a form of the word, printed
  `esemplo` a line above; the 1933 edition reads `esemplo`. Recommendation:
  emend `es[e]mplo` under D68, as D381, **D396**.
- p. 57 l. 8 `da principali magistrati ⟨e|o⟩ nel quale`: A prefers `e` but
  reads the letter clearly as o; B reads `o`; the collator saw `o`. A
  word-level difference at e/o (D203(b)). The clause is a second relative on
  `il populo romano`, and `o` does not construe; the 1933 edition reads `e`.
  Recommendation: emend `[e]` under D68 and D126, **D397**.

**Doubtful to both, the alternatives words.** `tanti e ⟨si|sì⟩ noti`: both
prefer `si`; only `sì` construes; the 1933 edition reads `sí`.
Recommendation: `sì`, **D398**.

**Against the 1933 edition (to David).**
