# Pair I.10 — pass A against pass B

All five readers were dispatched together, each with a directory holding only
its page images, and returned only boundaries, counts and paths. Unlike
earlier pairs, pass A was also a subagent rather than the drafting session:
both passes were cold, and the collator had seen neither page before the
passes came back. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini
pages rendered at 1200 ppi in halves, with enlarged quarter-page crops; the
Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in thirds.

## NM-DISC.1.10 — 1824, pp. 53–58

1,198 words (pass B; pass A counts 1,200, splitting two page-joined words).
**No word, clause or sense disagreement**: after Class A the two token
streams are identical. Both passes find the same boundaries (CAPITOLO X under
the running head on p. 53, drop capital T; `sempiterna infamia.` at p. 58
l. 3; CAPITOLO XI follows on the same page), the same italic rubric with a
full stop, one printed block, no notes. The passes differ only in which
specks they mark: pass A marks seven (under `e spauriti`, after `Marco`,
over `Severo`, under `discordi`, `mancati`, `si`, and a faint final `e` in
`angustie`), pass B three (`ignorantemente`, `Marco`, `Severo`); both read
the same letters at every one, and none is carried. Both pass the spacing
fault `igno rantemente` and join it.

## Comparison witnesses

Witness independence (SCOPE §7): the passes split at the two spelling loci
above, one each, both u/o misreadings on a scan that breaks u. Pass A's
`volontà` at .03 is not the 1933 reading (`voluntá`), and pass B's
`pericolosa` is. No pattern; neither pass carries any other Palmarocchi
reading listed below.

**FG, 1933 edition.** Readings that differ in word from Canestrini, for the
variant notes: `immanissimi` for `inumanissimi`; `E' quali` for `I quali`;
`in esso` for `in essa`; `credo che ve l'abbia` for `credo ve l'abbia`;
`quello che si porta` for `quello che li porta`; `le tirannide`,
`immaginazione`; `pena [di] rebellione`, bracketed; and the two `È` of D181.
Everything else is Palmarocchi's orthographic profile (`El titolo`, `el`,
`potenzia`, `reverenzia`, `pericolo`, `popoli`, `maraviglia`, `adunche`,
`Lacedèmone`) and his heavier pointing, and is not enumerated.

**NM, 1554 Giglio.** A third reader transcribed fol. 19v–21v cold
(`NM-DISC.1.10-1554.md`); the transcript was collated by word against the
established 1824 text, spelling normalised (u/v, h, -ti-/-zi-, doubled
consonants, `&`). The readings cited below were checked on the page image by
the collator, except `minutamente`. The differences that bear on the
English:

- .01 `degni di maladitione` for `detestabili`; `alle genti humane` for
  `alla umana generazione`; no `e i da pochi`.
- .03 `o che per fortuna`; `di gran lunga` for `di lunga`.
- .04 `spauentati` for `spauriti`; `uitupereuole` for `detestabile`;
  `celebrarono` (past) for `celebrano`.
- .06 `Purgasi` for `Pongasi`, a misprint (the catchword reads `è tempo`); no
  `e dipoi` before `elegga`.
- .07 `minutamente` for `tritamente`.
- .08 `preposte` for `proposte`; `continoue molestie` for `continue angustie`.

The numbers in .05 are printed as numerals (`XXVI`, `XVI`, `X`) and agree.
