**O20.** The printed final letter is a-shaped. So is every barless e on this
scan: I.3 prints `ara` for `era` twice, and this unit prints `plabe`,
`aletti`, `avvartito` for `plebe`, `eletti`, `avvertito`. `nuova leggi` is
not Italian (broken agreement). Recommend reading `nuove` as a damaged e.
If you read the sort as a real a, it is a compositor's error and D68 emends
it to `nuov[e]` from the 1933 edition; the English is "new laws" either way.
