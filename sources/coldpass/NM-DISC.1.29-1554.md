# Reader's notes

Conventions. Line breaks as printed. Long s (ſ), u/v, & , æ and accents kept as printed. Line-end hyphens are rendered "-" where a break mark is visible in the image (the press uses short raised dots/dashes, often faint); where no mark is visible at a word break the line is left without one (e.g. 44r "quel / lo", 44v "quel / lo", "miglio / ri", 45r "ha / ueua", "l'ani / mo", "Scipio / ne", "Sci / pione", "dal / la", "pru / d[en]za", "acquiſta / uano", "Tal / che"). Spacing around punctuation normalised to single spaces; the printing has many spaced commas/stops, which are kept where clearly spaced.

Folio inference. Pages 2 and 4 are rectos numbered 44 and 45 with running head PRIMO.; pages 1, 3, 5 are unnumbered versos with running head LIBRO. Hence page 1 = 43v, page 3 = 44v, page 5 = 45v. The running head on 43v is badly inked ("LIBRO" with the L looking like a B/D).
