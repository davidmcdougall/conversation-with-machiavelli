## Reader's notes

- l. 6 ⟨premiato|preniiato⟩: the letters after "pre" look in the scan like "niia" (m broken/thinned into n + i); almost certainly a worn or poorly rendered "m", but recorded as uncertain.
- l. 4 "cittadini ," and other commas: the scan shows wide spacing before several commas (typical of the typesetting); spacing not reproduced, punctuation is.
- Semicolons (ll. 2, 11, 22, 26, 27) have a comma-like lower element; read as ";".
- Apostrophes in s’aggiunge (l. 13), l’uno (l. 26), l’altro (l. 28) printed as curved apostrophes, followed by a thin space in the print; transcribed closed up.
- Accents all grave as printed: più, è, cancellò, Città, rovinerà, Perchè, diventerà, risolverà, civiltà, benchè, perchè, sarà, tantochè; capital È (ll. 17, 25) printed with grave.
- "premj" (ll. 5, 19) printed with j.
- l. 23–24 "ancora che / grande" read as printed (sense might expect "ancora che piccolo"/"non grande"); not corrected.
- l. 23 "bene," — comma sits close to the word; clear.
- l. 29 "Re" capital R, "delli" — the d is slightly broken but clear.
- Small marks in the gutter/left margin and a speck right of l. 6 are not text.
