# Reader's notes

- Heading: "CONSIDEAAZIONE" as printed; the R is blotted into an A-shape. Read Considerazione (same word is clean in the next heading).
- Heading: the period after XXX is clearly printed.
- Rubric l.1, "a una repubblica": the letter looks like a, but it is small and could be a broken o. Preferring what's printed (a). Flag for collation.
- Rubric l.2, "ingratitudina": final letter looks like a; probably a broken e. Read ingratitudine.
- Rubric l.2, punctuation after ingratitudin-: a colon-like mark, partly smudged; could be ; . Prefer :.
- Rubric l.2, second "quel": a mark sits above the u (qûel), most likely a speck. Read quel.
- Rubric l.3, "assere": the first letter looks like a; probably e broken. Read essere.
- Rubric l.3, final period after "quella": faint, possibly absent.
- Body l.2, "reputazione ,": there is a visible space before the comma. Kept as printed; flagged because the spacing may be a scan artefact.
- Body l.3, "tntti": u broken into n. Read tutti.
- Body l.3, "snoi": u broken into n. Read suoi.
- Body l.3, "lc": barless e. Read le.
- Body l.4, "capitam": the final i and its dot are fused with the m-shape. Read capitani (the word is clean on l.9).
- Body l.4, punctuation after capitani: looks like " ;" with a space before it; the upper dot is faint, so it could be a comma. Prefer ;.
- Body l.4, "aia": s blotted into a. Read sia.
- Body l.6, "frntto": u broken into n. Read frutto.
- Body l.6, "ano": u read as a. Read uno (cf. "a uno tiranno" l.5).
- Body l.7, "E ne": a mark sits above the capital, which could be a grave accent (È) or a speck. Prefer plain E (conjunction, "And we see it ...").
- Body l.7, "de` prin-": the apostrophe is printed reversed, like a backtick. Read as an apostrophe; the elided form stays separated by a space.
- Body l.8, "comnnemente": u broken into n. Read communemente. Read it as communemente, not comunemente: there are two m-shaped strokes before the "nn".
- Body l.9, "rarisaime": s blotted into a. Read rarissime.
- Body l.10, "aimstri": the s is blotted and the in reads as m. Read sinistri.
- Footnote 1, "fuggira": final letter a-like. Read fuggire.
- Footnote 1, "necesaità": s blotted. Read necessità.
- Footnote 1, "cha": barless e / a confusion. Read che.
- Footnote 1, final period after "virtuosi": faint or absent.
- Not counted as uncertain: "perchè" (the grave accent is legible), "dì" (accent present), "Discorso" capitalised as printed, "Stato" capitalised as printed.
