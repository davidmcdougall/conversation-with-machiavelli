# Reader's notes

- Convention: the 1824 type sets a thin space before commas and semicolons throughout; not recorded. Apostrophe spacing is recorded as seen.
- Page, l. 11 "discorse": a small raised mark sits over the s (looks like an accent or a speck); word itself is clear. Recorded as ⟨discorse|discorśe⟩; I take it as a speck.
- Page, l. 20 "l'ingratitudine": the gap after the apostrophe is slightly wider than in "l'uno", "l'altro", "l'animo" (all closed); could be a deliberate space. Preferred closed.
- Page, l. 16 "all' uno": clear space after the apostrophe (recorded, not a locus).
- Page, l. 10 "Vero": a speck touches the top of the V; not a letter or mark. Not counted.
- Page, l. 8 "vive": a dot over the first v is a speck. Not counted.
- Page, l. 7-8: no hyphen issue; "Repub- / blica" hyphenated at line end as printed.
