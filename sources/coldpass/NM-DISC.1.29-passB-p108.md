# Reader's notes

- Line 25 (Vitellio, il quale regnava a Roma, e virtuosissi-): immediately after "e" there is a small raised mark, a short vertical tick sitting high, before the gap preceding "virtuosissi-". It is thinner and shorter than the apostrophe in "ogn'industria" (l. 17) or "d' Antonio" (l. 28), so I read it as a speck or broken sort rather than an apostrophe; but it is positioned exactly where an apostrophe would sit. Preferred "e", alternative "e'".
- Line 28 (d' Antonio): the apostrophe is a clear comma-shaped raised mark; a visible space follows it before "Antonio", unlike "ogn'industria" (l. 17), where no space follows. Preferred reading keeps the space; flagged only because the gap is narrow.
