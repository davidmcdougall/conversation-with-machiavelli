# Pair I.29 — cold passes and collation

The unit-twenty review of D199 came first: David ruled D302 before any
reader was dispatched. NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were
then dispatched together. Under D302 the NM sample page waited for pass A and
the Giglio read, was chosen by the density of word-level 1554 differences,
and went to a fifth reader alone, with line crops of every word-level 1554
difference outside it. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the
Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi, whole and in halves; the Giglio leaves at
500 ppi in thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages against the passes, and
checked the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf images.

`fatto`/`fatta`); the tie between pp. 108 and 109 breaks to p. 108 on the
total count. So the sample was read twice: p. 110 by the first pass-B reader
(`NM-DISC.1.29-passB.md`), and p. 108, the page the rule chooses, by a
sixth reader alone (`NM-DISC.1.29-passB-p108.md`).

With the p. 110 reader went thirteen crops of one to six lines carrying
every word-level 1554 difference outside p. 110 and pass A's two doubts
