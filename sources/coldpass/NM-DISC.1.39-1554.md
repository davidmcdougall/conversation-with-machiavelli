# Uncertain loci

1. fol. 57r, body l. 5: "ch'eglie" printed as one unit before "facil"; could be read as "ch'egli e" with a tight space. The letters are clear; the word break is not. (A stray dot between "facil" and "coſa" is taken as dirt.)
2. fol. 57r, body l. 11: punctuation after "legge" looks like a semicolon with a dot beside it (";."); could be a colon. Preferred ";".
3. fol. 57r, body l. 28: "fecero" carries a mark over the final o that may be a grave accent ("fecerò") or a blemish.
4. fol. 57v, body l. 7: "leuo" — no clear accent on the final o, unlike "leuò" on 57r l. 31; an accent may be worn.
5. fol. 57v, body l. 10: "doueuono" — the penultimate vowel reads as o; "doueuano" would be the expected form, and an a/o confusion is possible in this italic.
