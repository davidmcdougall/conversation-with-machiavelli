## FG-CONS.1.2 — Canestrini, pp. 6–10

1,240 words. **No clause or sense disagreement.** Both passes find five
printed paragraphs at the same points, the same four editor's notes (not part
of the unit), and no stop after the closing `Discorso`.

The scan loses the crossbar of e throughout, so e and o are the recurring
doubt. Pass B marks some forty letters for it; at every locus but the two
below, the two passes read the same word.

Escalated, and ruled by David 2026-09-28:

| Locus | Pass A | Pass B | 1933 | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| .03 `la ruina ___ città` | `della` | `⟨dello⟩` | `delle` | `delle` (D71): the o is a barless e, as B's `lo cose` for `le cose` on the same page |
| .05 `___ la mistizia` | `sarà` | `s'⟨?⟩arà` | `s'ará` | `s'arà` (D72): the printed space is a dropped apostrophe, as D39 |

Common doubt, not a split: `o dove` (.01). Pass A marked it e|o, pass B read
`o`. At magnification the letter has no bar where the e of `avvertire` in the
same line has one. Established `o`; the 1933 edition's `e dove` goes in the
variant note (D38).

**Stops.** At eleven sentence breaks — a capital after a wide space, or a
paragraph end — neither pass sees a point, and the 1933 edition prints one:
after `male` (.02), `ricordassi`, `pericolo`, `breve`, `limitatissima`,
`facilmente` (.03), `cattivi`, `dominio` (.04), `universale` (.05),
`turbazione`, `commessa`. Supplied as witness corrections, as in I.1 (D67).
None at the closing `Discorso` (DEC-009 clause 10).

Six further wide spaces carry no mark in either pass and are not supplied
(`regia`, `annua`, `sedizione`, `modo`, `il male`, `sicura`); the 1933
edition points four of them. One of them matters to the parse: `e fuggire il
male che gli ottimati non siano` reads more easily with Palmarocchi's comma,
since the `che` clause is the content of `Bisogna`, not a relative on `male`.
The English carries that reading and a translator's note says so.

Class A (accents, apostrophes, the plural article `e'`, apocope `de'`/`da'`)
applied by rule and not enumerated.
