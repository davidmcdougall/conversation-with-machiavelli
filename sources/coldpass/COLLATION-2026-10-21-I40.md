# Pair I.40 — cold passes and collation

All five readers were dispatched together, each given its own image
directory and its own output folder (the I.39 isolation failure is not
repeated). The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi, whole and in thirds; the
Canestrini pages at 1200 ppi (native 600), whole and in halves; the Giglio
leaves at 500 ppi, whole and in thirds. Every reader found the unit's
boundaries for itself. The collator read the 1933 pages against the passes
and checked the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf images.

`fazione` occurs in neither unit.

## NM-DISC.1.40 — 1824, pp. 142–149

1,814 words (both passes: p. 142 76, p. 143 250, p. 144 246, p. 145 243,
p. 146 248, p. 147 257, p. 148 255, p. 149 239). Both passes find
CAPITOLO XL on p. 142 below the last line of XXXIX (`ai Dieci, così loro
ricrearono i Consoli.`), the four-line italic rubric, a drop initial V
(`Volendo discorrere`), one printed block with no notes, Livy's Latin set in
italic within the text (pp. 144–145), and the end at p. 149 body line 29
`occupare la Tirannide.`, the rest of the page blank; CAPITOLO XLI opens
p. 150. The printer's signature `MACHIAVELLI V. I` / `10` at the foot of
p. 145 is not text.

**No sample (D352).** D302 requires the sample page to hold at least a fifth
of the chapter. The fullest page, p. 147, holds 257 words, 14%; no page is
eligible. Pass B therefore read the whole chapter, the rule's ordinary course
before D199. Nothing in D302 is changed by this.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The 2,064 word and
punctuation tokens are identical between the passes after Class A; the only
differences the diff throws up are two italic line-end hyphenations
(`feren-dae`, `liberta-tem`) joined differently by the script. Pass A marks
29 loci, pass B 20. Doubts common to both readers are letter-forms with no
word for an alternative (`Fece`|`Fcce`, `arripuit`|`aripuit`, `esse
azioni`|`esseazioni`) or marks both read alike (`città:`, `e'`, the specks
after `proposito.`, `oppressione`, `cosa,`, `romano,`). Pass A's `eum`|`cum`
(Livy, p. 145) is read `eum` without doubt by pass B. Pass B's rubric
`salvare per` is spacing.

**Against the 1554 (D203(b)).** Four word-level differences at letters the
scans break, each read by both readers without doubt, came to David:

- p. 143 `per fermare nuove leggi` and `esaminare e fermare dette leggi` /
  1554 `formare` (e/o), both.
- p. 144 `per poterlo innanzi alla confermazione loro emendare` / `poterle`
  (o/e): the 1824's pronoun takes the defect, the 1554's the laws.
- p. 147 `non gliene arebbono potuto mantenere` / `potuta` (o/a).
- p. 144 `tutta la Nobiltà` / `nabilta` (o/a), a 1554 misprint.

All kept, **D353**.

**Whole words bearing on sense.** Read by both readers without doubt, no
D203 rule triggers; raised by the collator, as D305:

- p. 143 `che egli si aveva presupposto di stabilire` / 1554 `presupposto
  stabile`.
- p. 146 `due eserciti, guidati da parte di detti Dieci` / `guardati`.
- p. 147 `ed amico di quelli che non erano concorsi` / `& nemico`, which
  breaks the period's antithesis.
- p. 148 `che a tutti satisfaccia` / `a tutta`, the nobility.

All kept, **D354**.

Recorded as variant notes without ruling, each read by two readers and
none bearing on a ruling: the 1554 follows each Latin quotation from Livy
with an Italian rendering introduced by `Cioè`, which the 1824 does not
print; it omits `Da questo i` before `Dieci presono occasione`; it reads
`seguire il magistrato` for `continuare nel Magistrato`, `per homicida`
for `per omicidio`, `il suo popolo` for `il Popolo`, `lo` for `questo
modo` (`se lo haueſſe tenuto`), and `dap loro` for `da per loro`. The rest
is form and spelling (`souerchio`, `X`, `XII`, `CXX`, `duoi`, `delli`,
`tra`, `Prencipe`, `merauiglia`, `romore`, `oppenione`, `nemico` for
`inimico` throughout, `onde` for `donde`, `essendo` for `sendo`, and the
`-ßino`, `-orno` verb forms).

## FG-CONS.1.40 — Canestrini, pp. 51–52

365 words (A 365, B 365). Both passes find the heading CONSIDERAZIONE SUL
CAPITOLO XL at the head of p. 51 under the running head, with nothing of the
preceding reply on the page; a three-line rubric in smaller type, hanging;
two indented paragraphs, `Io mi persuado` to `occupare la libertà.` (p. 51
l. 18) and `E quanto alla dottrina generale` to `occorrenze che girano.`
(p. 52 l. 11); then CONSIDERAZIONE SUL CAPITOLO XLVII on the same page. No
note of Canestrini's belongs to the reply; the note on p. 52 is keyed to
the XLVII reply. `paragraph_divisions: witness` (D66). A marks 25 loci,
B 34.

**Body: no word-level split.** The 408 tokens are identical after preferred
readings, but for the accent on `vòlti` (A grave, B circumflex): Class A.

**Rubric: two splits between words.**

- `in essa ⟨è|e⟩ da notare`: A prefers `è`, B `e`, neither sees the accent
  clearly. `e` does not construe; the 1933 edition and Machiavelli's 1824
  rubric read `è`. Read `è`, **D355**.
- `si considera, ⟨intra|mira⟩ molte altre cose`: A prefers `intra`, B the
  printed shapes `mira`; `mira` does not construe; the 1933 edition reads
  `intra`. Read `intra`, **D355**.

**Doubtful to both, the alternatives words.**

- `più gagliardo, ⟨e|a⟩ che non varia` (p. 52 l. 6): both prefer `e`;
  `a` does not construe; the 1933 edition reads `e`. **D356**.
- `non varia di animo ⟨si|sì⟩ facilmente` (p. 52 l. 7): both readers see no
  accent and prefer the printed `si`; the sense wants the adverb, "so
  readily", and the 1933 edition prints `sí`. The scan drops fine strokes
  (the rubric's `è`). Read `sì`, **D356**.

The other common doubts are letter breakage with no word for an alternative
(`facessi`, `mentre`, `con`, `fussino`, `meglio`, `causa`, `che`,
`conclusione`) or apostrophes (`d'Atene`, `nell'altra`, D39). Pass B's
single doubts (`populo` ×2, `ciascuna`, `umori`, `essere`, `varia`) are
u/n and s/a breakage read alike by A.

**Against the 1933 edition.** No word-level difference at a letter the scan
breaks, and none bearing on sense. The rest is his orthographic profile and
forms (`el`, `popolo`, `legge`, `nobilità`, `ed`, `ed e' compagni`,
`truovano` ×2, `ragione`, `cagione leggiere`, `imprudenzia`, `occorrenzie`).
His rubric, bracketed as his, reads `si può o salvare, per simile
accidente`; Canestrini's has no `o`, read by both readers without doubt:
same sense, recorded only.
