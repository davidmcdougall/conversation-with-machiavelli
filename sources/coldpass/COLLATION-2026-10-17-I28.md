# Pair I.28 — cold passes and collation

NM pass A, FG passes A and B and the Giglio read were dispatched together.
The NM sample page was chosen after pass A came back and given to a fifth
reader alone. The 1824 pages went at 350 ppi in thirds; the Canestrini pages
at 1200 ppi in halves enlarged 1.6x, with quarter-page crops enlarged 2.2x;
the Giglio leaves at 500 ppi in thirds. The collator read the 1933 pages
against the passes, and checked the 1554 readings cited below on the leaf
images.

## NM-DISC.1.28 — 1824, pp. 105–106

417 words (pass A: p. 105 197, p. 106 220; elided forms counted apart). Pass
A finds CAPITOLO XXVIII at the head of p. 105 under the running head, the
two-line italic rubric `Per qual cagione i Romani furono meno ingrati ai
loro cittadini che gli Ateniesi.`, a large initial Q, one printed block, no
notes, and the end at p. 106 line 23 `nel seguente capitolo.`; the rest of
p. 106 is blank and CAPITOLO XXIX opens p. 107.

**The sample (D199, D203(c)).** Pass A marked 3 loci: 2 on p. 105, 1 on p.
106. P. 105 carries 197 words of the chapter (47%, at least a fifth) and has
the most pass-A doubts. The collator chose p. 105 and recorded the choice
before giving it out. Pass B read only that page.

**Result: no word, clause or sense disagreement.** The token streams of p.
105 are identical after Class A, except that pass B carried a doubt across
the line end at `d' ingratitu-|dine` (space after the apostrophe, Class A).
Pass A's two doubts there (`Qualunque`/`Qualunquc`, `fatte`/`fatie`) have
no word for an alternative; pass B shares the first. The full pass B is not
triggered, and the sample rule stands for the next chapter.

**Outside the sample (D203(a)).** Pass A's one doubt on p. 106 is
`che`/`ehc`: no word for an alternative. Nothing to raise.

**Against the 1554 (D203(b)).** At letters the scans break the word diff
finds two differences of word:

- .03 `ogni altra violenza che ... da quella Città fu fatto` (p. 105, the
  sample, both readers without doubt): 1554 `fu fatta` (o/a), checked on
  fol. 42v. As printed the participle does not agree with `violenza`, the
  antecedent of `che`. Emended `fatt[a]` under D68, **D295**.
- .04 `per la diversità degli accidenti che in questa Città nacquero` (p.
  106, pass A without doubt): 1554 `in queste città` (a/e), checked on fol.
  42v. Construes (the city is Athens); kept, D38, **D296**.

The rest are spellings of one word (`contro`/`contra`, `ricuperata`/
`recuperata`, `augumento`/`augmento`, `arebbe`/`harebbe`).

**Raised although no rule requires it.** .05 `ne dirò quello ne occorrerà
nel seguente capitolo` (p. 106, pass A alone, without doubt): the 1554
prints `quello che occorrerà` (checked on fol. 43r). The 1824 construes as
an elliptical relative, `quello [che] ne occorrerà`; kept, D38, **D297**, as
D270.

## FG-CONS.1.28 — Canestrini, pp. 42–43

426 words (A and B, elided forms counted apart). Both passes find the
heading CONSIDERAZIONE SUL CAPITOLO XXVIII on p. 42 after the last three
lines of the reply on XXVI (`... e remedii estraordinarii e violenti.`;
pass A calls it the reply on XXVII, which Guicciardini did not write),
Canestrini's two-line rubric `Per qual cagione i Romani furono meno ingrati

## Comparison witnesses

**Witness independence (SCOPE §7).** At one split pass A agrees with the
1933 edition and pass B does not (`nobilità`); at the other the 1933 form is
the one word available. Neither pass carries a Palmarocchi word-reading
where Canestrini differs: both have `discordia`, `il governo`, `il populo`,
`concioni`, `le paci`, `deliberazioni importanti`, `delle nuove leggi`, `le
cose gravi`, `le arti populari`, `e in conseguenza`, `D'onde`, against the
1933 `differenzia`, `el governo`, `el popolo`, `concione`, `le pace`,
`deliberazione importante`, `della nuova legge`, `le cose grave`, `le arte
populare`, `ed in consequenzia`, `Donde`. The passes read Canestrini.

**FG, 1933 edition.** Word differences from Canestrini: `differenzia`
(D290), `della nuova legge` (D293), `contro agli` in the rubric; the rest is
Palmarocchi's orthographic profile (`el`, `-e` plurals in `concione`,
`pace`, `arte`, `deliberazione`, `grave`; `popolo`, `communemente`,
`consequenzia`, `ed`) and his pointing (`greci;`, `senato;`, `tirannide;`).
His rubric reads `Per quale cagione ... contro agli loro cittadini`,
bracketed as his.

**NM, 1554 Giglio.** A third reader transcribed fol. 42v–43r cold
(`NM-DISC.1.28-1554.md`); the transcript was collated by word against the
established 1824 text, and the readings below were checked on the leaves by
the collator. The differences that bear on the English:

- .03 `fu fatta` for `fu fatto` (D295).
- .04 `in queste città` for `in questa Città` (D296).
- .05 `quello che occorrerà` for `quello ne occorrerà` (D297).
- .01 `meno cagioni`, plural, for `meno cagione`.
- .03 `fiorito` for `florido`, "flowering" for "flourishing".
- .03 `aspra uendicatrice`, "harsh avenger", for `acerrima vendicatrice`,
  "most bitter avenger".
- .04 `Chi considera`, present, for `Chi considererà`, future; and `si
  uerra` (a misprint, most likely for `si uedra`) for `si vedrà`.
- .05 `fato esule`, a dropped t.
