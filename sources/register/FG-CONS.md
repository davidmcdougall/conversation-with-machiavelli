# Considerazioni intorno ai Discorsi del Machiavelli

## Extent and pairing boundary

The surviving work contains 39 separately headed Considerazioni. Their headings and the complete Palmarocchi/Wikisource contents establish a direct one-to-one chapter-level response map: 28 units address Book I; eight address Book II (including its proem); and three address Book III. No unit in the inspected edition is headed as responding jointly to multiple Discorsi chapters. The individual provenance-bearing records are in `relationships/FG-CONS/`. [WIKISOURCE-CONS]

The map covers Book I chapters 1–12, 14, 16, 23–26, 28–30, 32, 39, 40, 47, 49, 58, and 60; Book II proem and chapters 10, 12–15, 19, and 24; and Book III chapters 17, 19, and 24. This is the complete surviving response set, not evidence that Guicciardini intended those omissions as a finished selection. [WIKISOURCE-CONS; TRECCANI-MACH]

## Witness posture — settled, see SCOPE §4

Canestrini 1857 governs. Public domain by age; scan layer under Public Domain
Mark 1.0; reproducible in the corpus. Palmarocchi 1933 is collation evidence
only: its scan images are PD-marked, the Wikisource transcription is CC
BY-SA/GFDL, and Palmarocchi's editorial expression is consultation-only.

The predecessor census rated this work S1 and required a work package to
"identify the manuscript basis and select or construct the governing text
before translation." That requirement is void. This project is not a text of
record (SCOPE §1) and does not adjudicate against Bausi 2001 or the manuscript
tradition (SCOPE §8). Where the governing witness differs from modern critical
consensus, the difference is recorded in a note, not resolved.

## English translations — relevant, and not inputs

Grayson (OUP, 1965) and Atkinson & Sices (NIU Press, 2002) are both complete
and both copyrighted. Neither is ever used as a translation input. Consult
privately, after a draft is finished, as a calibration check only (SCOPE §1).

## Open bibliographic questions

These are open as scholarship, and none of them gates any stage of this project.

- The exact manuscript witnesses, shelfmarks and autograph status.
- Whether a modern critical edition supersedes Palmarocchi for the complete text.
- Grayson 1965's completeness, confirmed from the volume rather than the catalogue.
- An edition-based word count, and the 39 headings tested against Canestrini's
  page images as they are acquired (see links/pairing-map.md).
