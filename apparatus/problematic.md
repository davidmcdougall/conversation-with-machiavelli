# Problematic — what the translation must keep visible

Each unit has a structure that smoothing would bury. These are checked on every
chunk.

## Machiavelli, NM-DISC.1.12

**The argument is a single chain, and the conclusion is an indictment reached by
it, not asserted.** Religion must be kept uncorrupted → because every religion
rests on some principal order of its own → which the prudent maintain and increase
*however false they judge it* → the Roman Church has not done this → therefore two
charges. Any rendering that lets the Church attack float free of the instrumental
argument about religion has lost the chapter.

**The instrumental irony must survive.** Machiavelli advises princes to favour and
increase what they privately judge false, and then condemns the Church for
corruption. He does not flag the tension. Neither does the translation.

**The two charges are numbered and must stay numbered.** `due potentissime` — the
first moral (example destroys devotion), the second political (division). This is
also where the 1554 witness transmits a different argument entirely (D006), so the
structure is doing evidentiary work as well as rhetorical.

**`favorirle ed accrescerle` → `favorita e accresciuta`.** Prescribed of princes in
.02, then reported of Camillus in .03. The echo is the demonstration: Rome did what
the chapter recommends. Both must land in the same English words.

**`propinqui` → `propinquo`.** Those nearest the Roman Church have least religion;
ruin or scourging is near. The repetition turns proximity from a blessing into a
threat within two sentences.

## Guicciardini, FG-CONS.1.12

**He concedes the premise and refuses the inference.** Not disagreement. The
movement is: intensify the condemnation of the Roman court beyond Machiavelli's →
accept as credible that the Church prevented an Italian monarchy → suspend
judgment on whether that was good or bad → distinguish republic from kingdom →
add counterweights → withhold easy concurrence. A translation that makes him
sound simply opposed destroys the unit, and with it the edition's argument.

**The acts of judgment are distinct and must not merge into one hedge.** `credo`,
`non so già`, `reputo`, `non concorro facilmente` are assent, suspension, judgment
and non-concurrence. Four different things.

**The hinge is `io non concorro facilmente`.** Ruled in SCOPE §3: *I do not readily
concur*. It answers Machiavelli's `discorrere` / `occorrono` directly, and the
whole terminology policy exists to protect that answer.

**He never resolves it.** The unit ends mid-thought, with no full stop in the
witness. The English does not supply a conclusion the Italian withholds.

## Machiavelli, NM-DISC.1.2

**The cycle is an argument, not a catalogue.** Six forms, each sliding into its
neighbour, and at every step the cause is inheritance; the mixed state is the
conclusion drawn from it, and Rome reaches it by accident, not by a founder.
Splitting the long periods of .04–.06 into a list of stages loses the causes
that join them.

## Guicciardini, FG-CONS.1.2

**He grants the conclusion and changes the subject.** Not disagreement: the
mixture is conceded in the first sentence, and the reply becomes a design. The
design runs through chains of `bisogna ... che` + subjunctive (.04, .05), and
every link must stay attached to its governor. Venice, not Rome, is his model;
the Romans' annual consuls and patrician optimates count against them.

## The pair

Machiavelli argues towards a demonstration. Guicciardini declines to be
demonstrated to. Read side by side, that is the book. Every chunk is checked
against whether it still reads that way.
