# SCOPE — A Conversation with Machiavelli

**A Conversation with Machiavelli: Guicciardini's *Considerazioni* on the
*Discorsi*** — a free, bilingual, hyperlinked edition of Guicciardini's 39
responses and the *Discorsi* chapters they answer. 78 text units, Italian and
English facing, every reading traceable to a named public-domain witness.

**Version.** Draft 0.3 — 2026-09-18. Nothing here is frozen; this file is
revised as the work teaches us things.

---

## 1. What this is, and what it is not

It **is** a reading edition with a transparent apparatus: the Italian
established from named public-domain witnesses, an English translation, notes
where the argument or the vocabulary needs them, and navigation that puts
Machiavelli's chapter and Guicciardini's reply on one screen.

It **is not** a text of record. It does not adjudicate the critical text
against Bausi 2001 or the manuscript tradition, and does not claim to. Where
our witnesses differ from modern critical consensus, we say so in a note.

**On the title.** Machiavelli had been dead two years when Guicciardini wrote;
the *Discorsi* were unpublished; the *Considerazioni* stayed unprinted until
1857. Machiavelli never answers. "Conversation" is therefore a deliberate
quotation, not a description: it invokes Machiavelli's letter to Vettori of
10 December 1513, where he changes into court dress at evening, enters "le
antique corti delli antiqui huomini," questions the ancients on the reasons for
their actions, and reports that *"quelli per loro humanità mi rispondono."*
Guicciardini does to Machiavelli what Machiavelli did to Livy — and is denied
the reply Machiavelli claimed. That asymmetry is the edition's argument and
belongs in the first paragraph of the introduction. (Establish the Vettori text
from a witness like everything else here; do not quote it from memory.)

**Honest positioning.** Two complete English translations already exist:
Margaret Grayson in *Francesco Guicciardini: Selected Writings* (OUP, 1965),
and James B. Atkinson & David Sices, *The Sweetness of Power* (NIU Press,
2002) — the same pairing, and the strongest comparator in the field.

The claim is not priority. It is **form and access**. Both existing versions
are copyrighted print books presenting the pairing linearly. None of the
following exists anywhere:

- a free and openly licensed English *Considerazioni*;
- the Italian and the English side by side;
- the pairing as navigation rather than page order;
- an apparatus exposing which witness governs each reading.

Smaller than "first," and true.

**Never use Atkinson/Sices or Grayson as a translation input.** Consult
privately, after a draft is finished, as a calibration check only.

## 3. The per-unit pipeline

Six stages. A unit is done when it is on the web; nothing is "done" at an
intermediate stage.

1. **Acquire** — page images from both witnesses.
2. **Transcribe & normalise** — Italian with provenance front-matter, per
   DEC-009. Dual independent cold pass; disagreement escalates, never reruns.
3. **Collate** — governing vs comparison witness. *This output becomes the
   published variant notes.*
4. **Translate** — English draft against the established Italian.
5. **Annotate** — argument notes, terminology notes, cross-references to the
   paired unit. *Content, not overhead.*
6. **Publish** — live, versioned, dated.

**The governing rule:** if a stage's output cannot be published to a reader,
the stage does not exist. Collation notes become variant notes. Witness
decisions become the source note at the foot of each chapter. Translation
difficulties become translator's notes. Link records become hyperlinks.
Nothing is produced for an audience of one.

### Terminology policy — first entry

**Preserve the *currere* family wherever English allows.** The quarrel is
conducted in verbs of running and Latin gave English the same stems.
Machiavelli *discorre* ("voglio contro ad essa discorrere quelle ragioni che
mi occorrono"); the reasons *occorrono* to him; Guicciardini answers *"io non
concorro facilmente."* Render that **"I do not readily concur,"** not "I am
not easily persuaded." Three *currere* compounds — discourse, occur, concur —
survive intact into English, and most translators sever them without noticing.

*Considerare* stands outside that family: not a motion verb but, on the
traditional derivation, *con-* + *sidus*, the augur's word for observing the
constellation whole before acting. Flag it as "traditionally derived from" —
the etymology is widely repeated but not secure.

### Paragraphing policy

**Paragraph divisions in this edition are editorial.** Both I.12 witnesses print
their unit as one unbroken block — established by measuring the left edge of
every line on every page, with no indent anywhere after the opening — and there
is no reason to expect the remaining 76 units to differ. The divisions in
`corpus/` are ours. They exist because a thousand unbroken words is punishing on
a screen, which is a good enough reason for a reading edition and not a good
enough reason to leave them undeclared.

They are load-bearing as well as cosmetic: `FG-CONS.1.12.01` and its siblings
are the unit addresses, the collation tables' locators point at them, and the
site's navigation is built on them. So they are declared here, marked
`paragraph_divisions: editorial` in each unit's front-matter, and stated in the
source note at the foot of each chapter. What is never done is to let the
apparatus imply a witness supports them.

The same applies to the chapter rubrics. Machiavelli's three-line italic rubric
is heading apparatus, not body text, and is carried in a `rubric` field rather
than inside the first paragraph.

## 5. Effort model

Per pair, once the pipeline is warm: transcribe & normalise ~30 min · collate
~20 min · translate 60–90 min · annotate ~30 min; acquire and publish
amortised into bulk and build steps.

≈2.5–3 hours of supervised work per pair. **39 pairs ≈ 100–120 hours.** One
pair a week is nine months; three a week is three months. Either is a real end
date, which is the point of a closed corpus.

Treat these as fiction until one pair has gone end to end. The first will take
a day and will correct every row.

## 7. The whole quality system

**One derived view.** `scripts/state` generates §2's table from unit
front-matter. There is no register, no PROJECT.md, no dashboard, no editorial
packet. The old repository failed not from too much governance but from four
state layers that disagreed, with the newest truth buried deepest and every
summary a reader reached first already stale. One generated view, and the
files beneath it.

**One validator**, written from scratch — around 150 lines — and run on every
commit, silent when clean:

1. Every text unit has a source line naming its governing witness.
2. Every footnote reference resolves.
3. Every *Considerazioni* unit points at a *Discorsi* unit that exists.
4. Every established reading is attested in a named witness under
   `sources/witnesses/`.

The old `scripts/validate` is not the starting point for this. Of its eight
checks only witness-independence is near anything on the list above; the rest
enforce machinery this file deletes. Lift logic from it where it happens to
fit, and write the file itself new.

**One human check:** reread a published unit cold three months later. Time is
a better reviewer than a second model pass.

**One inherited rule**, kept because it is genuinely load-bearing, and scoped
on 2026-09-18 to what it can actually catch: no image-derived reading enters the
text on a single model pass. A silent transcription error propagates into the
published text and nothing downstream catches it. Dual cold pass; disagreement
escalates to David, never reruns.

**The pass tests words, clauses and sense. Nothing else.** Does the second
reader drop anything, add anything, or read a word in a way that changes the
meaning? Those are the errors no later stage catches, because the translator
will faithfully render whatever is put in front of them.

Everything below that level is normalisation, not reading: accents, apostrophes
and elisions, capitalisation, punctuation, euphonic *d*, word division, space
after an elided apostrophe. Those are settled once in DEC-009 and applied
mechanically, and a divergence in them is not a finding. This edition does not
claim to be a text of record (§1) and does not adjudicate against the critical
tradition (§8); auditing orthographic fidelity to a nineteenth-century
compositor would be auditing a layer it has already disclaimed. Collation
reports name the normalisation classes in one line each and do not enumerate
instances.

**But check the comparison witness before you classify anything as
normalisation.** This is the one rule that sits above the class system, and it
exists because on 2026-09-21 the class system swallowed a witness failure whole.
FG-CONS.1.12's first pass agreed with Palmarocchi 1933 — the comparison
witness — at twenty-nine loci where it disagreed with Canestrini: bracketed
supplies, apostrophes, euphonic *d*, capitalisation, spelling, accents, seven
points of pointing. The dual pass detected every one. The collation then filed
them as "pass A's undeclared editorial conventions" and routed them to DEC-009,
where they sat for three days looking like an orthography question.

**A pass that agrees systematically with a witness that does not govern is not
exhibiting a convention. It is reading the wrong book.** So, before any
divergence is called normalisation: take the set of loci where the two passes
disagree and check it against the comparison witness. If the first pass lands
with the non-governing witness more often than chance, stop, and raise a
provenance finding rather than a normalisation question. The check costs one
pass over a list you already have. Not doing it cost an entire established
unit, its integrity class, and a DEC-010 licence breach, on the first pair the
project ever finished.

This rule is empirical, not a preference. `qc/source-integrity/` holds the
stress runs that produced it (WI-000062–64: sample manifests, metrics,
intervention logs, an independent rerun). The pilot document and one run's
results port with it, so the rule arrives carrying its evidence rather than as
an assertion.

**What makes a pass cold.** The second reader must not have seen the first
reader's output. This is the whole content of the rule and it is easy to violate
by accident, because the obvious way to check a transcription — open it and read
along against the page — destroys exactly the independence being tested. An
assistant that has the established text in its context cannot perform the second
pass, however careful it is.

**So the second pass always goes to a separate agent, by default, for this and
every review of the same kind.** It is given the page images in a directory
containing nothing else, and instructed not to open any other file, not to
search, and not to use the scan's OCR layer for any reading. Parallel readers get
separate scratch directories. Adjudication is a third role: the collator sees
both passes and neither page, and escalates disagreements rather than settling
them.

First run, 2026-09-18, on the I.12 pair — see `sources/coldpass/`. Across 1,419
words the two passes disagreed about no word, no clause and no sense. Every
divergence was orthographic or punctuational. The one reading carrying any
semantic weight, Canestrini's bracketed supply in `non [ar]ebbe patito`,
resolves on sense and on Palmarocchi's unbracketed `arebbe`: the second reader
missed thin brackets on a bitonal scan. The transcription was sound and the pass
said so.

That is the result to reason from, not the raw count. Run unscoped it produced
44 divergences, none of which would have changed an English word. Scoped as
above it is cheap, and a run that comes back empty is worth having. Revisit at
unit ten whether a clean witness can be sampled rather than fully
double-passed.

Everything else is deleted — Class T review of governance documents,
bounded-amendment rechecks, the E1–E7 gate, the freeze tag. **The edition is
versioned, not frozen.** Publish v0.3, be wrong, fix it, publish v0.4, and
date every page.

## 8. Explicitly out of scope

- The complete works of either author.
- *Il Principe*, the *Ricordi*, the *Storia d'Italia*, the legations, the
  correspondence, the literary works. The census map is kept, as a map.
- Adjudicating the critical text against Bausi 2001 or Harley MS 3533.
- Any claim to be an edition of record.
- External Italianist review as a gate. Solicit it after publishing, as
  feedback on something real, not as permission to begin.

The ambition to widen is written down nowhere in this repository, because
writing it down is what regrew the governance last time. Ship this book first.
